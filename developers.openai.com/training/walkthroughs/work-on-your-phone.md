# Working on your phone

Use ChatGPT Work from the mobile app, or use Remote to review, approve, and start tasks on your connected computer.

## Use ChatGPT Work on your phone

Open the ChatGPT app, choose Work, and start a task or pick up a
conversation where you left off. You can use connected plugins, attach
files, and review results right from your phone.

Ask ChatGPT to catch you up on Slack, prepare you for a meeting, or
pull together what needs your attention. Review the result and keep
the conversation moving wherever you are.

![ChatGPT Work on a phone summarizing Slack updates and surfacing decisions, customer impact, and next steps after time away](/images/codex/walkthroughs/mobile-work-back-from-pto.webp)

## What you can do from your phone

Your mobile app brings Work conversations, connected tools, and finished
results together.

Plugins
:   Use Slack, Gmail, Google Calendar, and other connected tools.

Files
:   Attach a file or review documents, spreadsheets, and presentations.

![](/images/codex/icons/sites.svg) Sites
:   Open a Site you created or shared and check it from your phone.

Work chats
:   Start a task or continue cloud work from another device.

## Start, approve, and review computer tasks with Remote

Open Remote to see your tasks, approve a requested action, review
results, or start a new task on your connected computer. Your computer
keeps working with its local files, desktop apps, and browser.

### Set up Remote

In the ChatGPT desktop app on your computer, go to Settings →
Connections → Control this Mac or PC, then select Set up or Add. Scan
the QR code with your phone and select your connected computer. Keep
your computer awake, online, and signed in to the same account and
workspace as your phone.
