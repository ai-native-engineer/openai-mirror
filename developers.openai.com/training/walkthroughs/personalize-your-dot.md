# Personalize your dot

Give your dot context, set expectations, and refine its work with feedback.

Your dot can help you keep work moving as plans change. Start as you would with a new assistant: explain the job, share the right information, and talk through what good work looks like. As you review its work together, turn what you learn into clearer guidance for next time.

## Dots, ChatGPT Work, and scheduled tasks

Your dot is one personal agent you can keep working with as your plans change. You reach the same dot in ChatGPT, Slack, or Teams, and through texting where available. Switching channels does not create a new dot or reset its memory; messages stay in their respective conversations. If you already use ChatGPT Work or scheduled tasks, here’s how a dot fits alongside them.

| Use | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| [ChatGPT Work](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/get-started-with-work) | You have a task with a clear result, such as researching a question, comparing options, or creating a file. You can review its progress and give further instructions. |
| [Scheduled tasks](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/automations) | You want work to run at a set time or repeat on a schedule, such as preparing a brief every Monday. Where supported, tasks can also run from an app event. |
| [Your dot](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots) | You want help keeping track of a responsibility, such as preparing for upcoming meetings and following up on open questions. Your dot can decide when to check back, follow up as things change, and ask when it needs your input. |

You can use them together. Scheduled tasks can run work you’ve delegated to ChatGPT Work, and your dot can delegate tasks to ChatGPT Work or set up scheduled checks. You can keep talking to your dot as priorities change.

Working together over time gives you chances to make expectations clearer. Explain why a draft helped, which detail was wrong, or why an update needed your attention. Your dot uses relevant ChatGPT memory and can save notes about preferences and decisions. Ask it to summarize guidance you want to reuse, review that summary, and check a later result to see whether it applied it.

Saved notes are not a complete conversation history. Repeat essential requirements and link to the current document. If you need a decision from an earlier ChatGPT conversation, ask your dot to find it and confirm the details.

[How context and memory work](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/dots/tasks-and-memory#context-and-memory)
