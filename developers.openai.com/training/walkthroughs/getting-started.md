## Chat or Work?

Use Chat when you need a thoughtful answer, want to explore an idea, or work through a question. Choose Work when a task needs information from other apps, several steps, or a finished result you can review.

Say you need to confirm a team dinner. The restaurant’s email is in Gmail, while the latest headcount and dietary restrictions are in Slack. Work can bring those details together and draft the reply.

Chat
:   Ask, explore, or think through an idea.

Work
:   Gather context from tools and prepare a result you can
    review.

 Why not use Work for everything?

Both Chat and Work can use plugins. Choose Chat for
questions, brainstorming, and quick edits. Choose Work when
you want ChatGPT to carry out a task with several steps and
bring back a result you can review.

Longer or more complex tasks may take more time and use more
credits. Start in Chat if a conversation is enough; choose
Work when those extra steps help. [Learn about Work usage](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/get-started-with-work#use-chatgpt-work-efficiently)
.

## Connect the right apps

Plugins connect ChatGPT to the apps you already use. Open Plugins in the sidebar, search for Gmail, and select Install. Do the same for Slack.

Once they’re connected, ask ChatGPT to find an email, get context from Slack, and draft a reply. Review the message, make changes, and send it when you’re ready.
