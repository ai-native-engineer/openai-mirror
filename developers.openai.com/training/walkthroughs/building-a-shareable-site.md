# Building a shareable site

Describe what you want, and ChatGPT can build and host a real website, even from ChatGPT Work on your phone. Create a dashboard, shared trip planner, interactive tool, game, or whatever else you can imagine.

## Turn an idea into a website

Start with an idea, or keep going in a conversation you already have.
Ask ChatGPT Work to make a website, and tell it what people should be
able to do there. ChatGPT handles the code and hosting.

The site can save information in a database, such as votes, expenses, or
form responses. It can also store uploaded files, like receipts, photos,
or documents. You can choose who is allowed to visit.

Imagine you’ve been planning a trip with friends in ChatGPT. You’ve
already chosen dates, found a place to stay, and collected reservations.
Mention  [![](/images/codex/icons/sites-composer-icon.svg) @Sites](https://chatgpt.com/sites)  in that conversation to turn the plan into a website the whole group can
use.

Sites is in public beta. If you don’t see it, check the current [availability and workspace requirements](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001339).

## Make changes in the browser

In the ChatGPT desktop app, open your site in the in-app [Browser](/training/walkthroughs/using-your-computer-and-browser). Leave a comment on the part you want to change, then ask ChatGPT to
make the edit in the same conversation. For the trip site, you might
say, “Move the itinerary above the map,” or “Let everyone upload a
receipt next to each expense.”

On the web, open the site’s preview, select **Edit**, and describe your changes. You can also return to the Work
conversation from your phone and keep asking for updates.

## Choose who can visit

A new site is limited to you and your workspace admins. Open **Sites**, find the site, and select **Share** to choose who can visit. The available options depend on your account and
workspace settings.

* **Owner and workspace admins**

  Keep the site limited while you review it.
* **Selected people or groups**

  Give access to particular teammates or groups.
* **Anyone in your workspace**

  Make the site available across your organization.
* **Anyone on the internet**

  Let anyone open the site, if public publishing is enabled.
