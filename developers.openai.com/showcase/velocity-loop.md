1. **Explore the menu concept**

   Explore a course-selection menu for a miniature car racing game.

   Generate a menu concept for a realistic, die-cast-style 3D racing game with loop-the-loop tracks and nitrous. Show five course choices, varying difficulty, three assist levels, and best-time records in a clear, readable layout.
2. **Explore the racing concept**

   Explore the on-track view, with orange loops and a readable racing HUD.
3. **Build the first course menu**

   Build course selection, assist controls, and visible time records.
4. **Build the first playable racer**

   Build five playable courses with nitro, timing, and personal-best ghosts.
5. **Research, implement and playtest**

   Refine handling, deterministic replays, and rendering through repeated playtests.
6. **Build a miniature workshop**

   Develop warmer workshop environments and more detailed cars with convincing motion.
7. **Generate the workshop materials**

   Generate a surface-material atlas for the modeled workshop.
8. **Add reasons to replay**

   Add race series and rewards for skillful driving and personal-best runs.
9. **Add a four-car garage**

   Add four distinct cars and balance their handling across the courses.
10. **Unify the cars and add shared competition**

    Keep car models consistent and add verified shared rankings.
11. **Refine the camera, nitro and track flow**

    Refine the camera, nitro, and track layouts for smoother racing.
12. **Finish the gameplay and interface**

    Polish race flow and loading while preserving records and older replays.
13. **Check the final mobile controls**

    Keep car choice, track choice, and Race accessible on phones.
