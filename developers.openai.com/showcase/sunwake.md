sunwake-the-last-light

![Generated First Light Voyage concept: a small boat crossing faceted blue waves toward sunrise](/showcase/sunwake/concept-first-light-voyage.webp)First Light Voyage · concept art for the water, lighting, and boat view.

1. **Generate the game concepts**

   Explore visual directions for a procedural sailing game.

   Generate a few concept images for a 3D boating game in different styles and game states. Show high seas and large waves at sunrise, with sunlight reflected on the water, sparkling blue spray, and droplets on the camera. The water can look retro and triangular, but movement and physics should feel extremely smooth. Aim for procedural geometry without imported sprites or models.
2. **Build the first sailing slice**

   Turn the selected concepts into a playable sailing prototype.
3. **Refine the sailing feel**

   Connect buoyancy, wakes, and spray to the boat’s movement.
4. **Explore two water treatments**

   Add a natural water style alongside the faceted default.
5. **Give the voyage structure and light**

   Add lighthouse destinations, weather, and a changing sky.
6. **Give the boat the same care**

   Build a detailed Blender boat with animated rigging and two material styles.
7. **Settle the boat naturally into the water**

   Adjust the boat’s waterline and buoyancy for natural contact with the sea.
