![Generated Tideglass Caverns concept: a small adventurer amid dark stone and glowing teal water](/showcase/hollowflux/tideglass-concept.webp)

1. **Explore visual directions**

   Generate five dungeon concepts with readable characters, fluid, and lighting.

   Generate five concept images for a top-down pixel dungeon crawler with real-time combat and a procedural fluid engine. Keep characters, equipment, and monsters simple and readable. Explore distinct environments with reactive water, blood particles, metal sparks, and responsive lighting. Aim for the atmosphere of Animal Well and the systemic variety of Caves of Qud, without ASCII.
2. **Build a playable encounter**

   Create a short combat encounter with code-drawn graphics and reactive water.
3. **Tune movement and attacks**

   Play the first encounter, then tune movement and attack animations.
4. **Add experience points**

   Add experience points so combat contributes to the player's progression.
5. **Refine the cape and facing**

   Keep the red cape readable and layer it correctly as the character turns.
6. **Connect water and electricity**

   Let electricity spread through connected water while stone stays safe.
7. **Make currents move objects**

   Let currents carry physical loot and debris through the dungeon's water.
8. **Build distinct floor themes**

   Expand the prototype into seven floor themes with a clear sense of descent.
9. **Add inventory and equipment**

   Let players store loot, inspect their gear, and equip items for the descent.
10. **Add boss encounters**

    Introduce bosses that test the combat and fluid rules learned on the way down.
11. **Build the sanctuary**

    Create a sanctuary where players can trade, buy upgrades, and make pacts.
12. **Keep the action readable**

    Keep the player and enemies easy to spot against water, lighting, and effects.
13. **Simplify the interface**

    Reduce interface clutter and make inventory and equipment easier to scan.
14. **Rebalance progression**

    Play through the descent and smooth out uneven difficulty and rewards.
15. **Refine the ambient sound**

    Tune the cavern ambience without masking important gameplay sounds.
16. **Give weapons distinct attacks**

    Make each of the six weapon families feel different to use in combat.
17. **Make weapon drops easy to compare**

    Show physical weapon drops with clear stat comparisons before pickup.
18. **Connect attacks to the water**

    Add lasting wakes, eddies, and foam that reflect each weapon's attacks.
