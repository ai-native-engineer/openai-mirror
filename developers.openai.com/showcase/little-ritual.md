little-ritual

![Generated cover illustration of the blue Little Ritual courier carrying four coffees through its warm miniature café neighborhood.](/showcase/little-ritual/cover-generated-sep4.webp)

1. **Build the coffee-delivery loop**

   Build a coffee-delivery game on a small, explorable spherical world.

   Build an original single-player 3D browser game about delivering coffee, set on a small, walkable spherical planet with a curved horizon. Give it a warm, stylized, cutesy look, mixing a compact town with quieter natural areas connected by paths around the globe. Include vertical exploration through stairs, upper floors, and bridges. Start the player in a café beside a coffee machine. Let them carry four visibly modeled coffees and explore to find neighbors, delivering to each once per round. Use a custom 3D interpretation of the Codex pet as the courier and other pets as neighbors. Keep discovery central, with interesting places to explore and small, playful interactions. Build the game modularly so we can change the world, characters, and mechanics easily. Use editable Blender assets, keep browser performance in mind, and begin with a playable version that we can improve through repeated feedback.
2. **Give the courier a rounded, painted look**

   Give the courier rounded forms, painted colors, and expressive movement.
3. **Build editable assets in Blender**

   Create editable Blender assets with named parts for game animation.
4. **Expand the town and its quieter corners**

   Expand the town with vertical routes, quiet natural areas, and distinctive places.
5. **Add characters and encounters**

   Give the world distinct inhabitants and playful encounters.
6. **Generate the studio mural**

   Create a painted mural texture for Sunroom Studio.
7. **Refine movement and mobile controls**

   Refine movement, navigation, and camera behavior across desktop and mobile.
8. **Optimize the game and render a trailer**

   Optimize the world, add original audio, and render a cinematic trailer.
