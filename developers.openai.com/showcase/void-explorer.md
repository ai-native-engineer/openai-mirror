void-explorer

![Generated orbital concept: a spacecraft approaching a ringed planet in cyan and magenta light](/showcase/void-explorer/concept-orbit.webp)

1. **Generate the game concepts**

   Explore a neon procedural universe with seamless travel from space to planets.

   Generate concept art for a fully explorable procedural universe where every star and planet I can see is somewhere I can go. I should be able to accelerate toward them, fly from space into a planet's atmosphere, and see the ground below. Use simpler retro 3D with retro-futurist neon colors, strong contrast, and an evocative sense of deep space. Find a middle ground between too realistic and too simplistic. Show orbital flight, atmospheric descent, and high-speed flight with stars and dust rushing around the ship.
2. **Build the first vertical slice**

   Build a playable universe with flight, reachable destinations, and planetary descent.
3. **Make flight and navigation feel right**

   Refine flight and make navigation visual with star charts and direct targeting.
4. **Upgrade the rendering**

   Upgrade lighting and planetary materials with WebGPU and WebGL support.
5. **Refine the spacecraft concept**

   Generate consistent reference views of AURORA’s four-wing spacecraft design.
6. **Build and integrate AURORA**

   Model AURORA in Blender and integrate the spacecraft into the game.
7. **Make exploration physical**

   Connect landing, walking, and reboarding into one planetary expedition.
8. **Keep the world coherent**

   Keep terrain, water, and atmosphere coherent throughout the journey.
