Plan an hourly itinerary and map with an agent, using a sample Notion itinerary as context. Edit or dismiss suggestions, leave comments, and export your trip as a PDF.

Your agent can turn travel plans from connected apps into an itinerary, then read your comments and revise the trip. WebMCP keeps that collaboration tied to the schedule and map you can see and edit.
