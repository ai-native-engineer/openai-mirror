## Use rollout data, surveys, and support signals to improve enablement after people start using ChatGPT Edu.

![Use Impact Data To Improve Your ChatGPT Edu Rollout](https://cdn.gradual.com/images/https://d2xo500swnpgl1.cloudfront.net/uploads/oaiacademy/Academy-content-covers-18--b30e4869-fa85-4d44-b7ec-b6f31f096b6e-1778084527526.jpeg?fit=scale-down&width=1200)

Review usage and survey signals regularly so your rollout gets better after launch.

A ChatGPT Edu rollout continues beyond provisioning seats and giving your campus access. Once people start using the workspace, rollout owners need to understand what is working, what is confusing, and where more support would help.

Impact data gives you a way to adjust enablement, communications, prompt packs, office hours, and stakeholder follow-up based on real signals.

## Review data frequently

Review a mix of signals:

* weekly active usage

* adoption by role or cohort

* support tickets and common questions

* office-hours attendance

* prompt pack or resource hub engagement

* survey feedback

* examples of time saved or work improved

* concerns about policy, privacy, or academic integrity

## Use Surveys As Rollout Checkpoints

Surveys can help you understand productivity impact, estimated hours saved, quality improvements, learning support, satisfaction, and creative exploration.

There are two survey paths to plan around:

* Admin-created surveys: Workspace owners can launch an impact survey at any time from Workspace analytics > Impact. Use this when you want a pulse at a specific moment, such as after a pilot, launch campaign, training series, department rollout, or major enablement push. Admin-created surveys are sent immediately after creation, so create one only when you are ready to launch it.

* OpenAI-created surveys: OpenAI-created impact surveys are scheduled at Day 120 and Day 240 after workspace creation, each with a 30-day response window. Treat these as lifecycle checkpoints for reviewing broader impact and updating your plan.

Learn more details about managing impact surveys [here](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001149-managing-impact-surveys-in-workspace-analytics).

## Turn Findings Into Action

For example:

* If faculty usage is low, add discipline-specific examples and a live teaching workflow session.

* If students are using ChatGPT mainly for writing, add responsible-use guidance and study workflows.

* If staff report time savings in communications, collect examples and publish a short campus story.

* If support tickets repeat the same access issue, update the resource hub and launch email.

* If advanced feature usage grows quickly, revisit credit alerts, limits, and pilot guardrails.
