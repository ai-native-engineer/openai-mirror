# Help Students Plan College, Careers, and Life with ChatGPT

Designed for: Academic and career advisors, coaches, and mentors.

Help students turn “I’m overwhelmed” or “What comes next?” into practical action. This 60-minute workshop explores how ChatGPT can enhance advising and mentoring with activities and practices that help students navigate coursework, campus life, and career goals more effectively.

Help students find new ways to organize competing commitments, prepare for an advising conversation, and connect their experiences to career planning and opportunities. Keep students involved in checking assumptions and choosing their next steps.

Leaving with ready-to-use resources such as example prompts, worksheets, or guides to help you empower students to plan and act with greater independence.

Beginners are welcome; follow along or watch and try later.
