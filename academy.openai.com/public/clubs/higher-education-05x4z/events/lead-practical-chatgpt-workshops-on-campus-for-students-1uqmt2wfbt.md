# Lead Practical ChatGPT Workshops on Campus for Students

Register

Designed for: Campus and IT trainers, librarians, academic technologists, and teaching and learning center staff.

Bring practical ChatGPT workshops to your library, learning center, orientation program, or campus training calendar. In this 60-minute session, discover ready-made ChatGPT workshops you can deliver to students tomorrow. Modular workshops cover personalizing ChatGPT, common student workflows, using ChatGPT to create responsibly, navigating academic success and life in college, and more. The approaches can be adapted for a class visit, student group, or drop-in conversation. Content designed for students with different levels of AI fluency.

At the end of the webinar, you’ll leave with resources such as example prompts, worksheets, presentations, and guides to make it easier to start empowering students with ChatGPT on your campus. Adapt what fits your audience and choose an opportunity to put it into practice.

You don’t need to be an AI expert or programmer; follow along live or watch and try later. Tool access and participation options will be explained before the session.
