# Help Students Learn and Create with ChatGPT

Designed for: Instructors, faculty, teaching assistants, and learning support teams.

Help students use ChatGPT to explore difficult ideas, develop projects, and improve their work. This 60-minute workshop offers approaches you can adapt across disciplines, in class, during tutoring, or in office hours.

Explore how students can partner with ChatGPT to personalize course materials, turn an idea into a presentation or website, and use feedback thoughtfully. You’ll get ideas about building in opportunities to check accuracy and explain their decisions, while keeping students responsible for the thinking and work they submit.

Leave with resources such as example prompts, assignments, rubrics, presentations, or assessment guides to help you empower your students to learn more deeply and create in expansive ways with ChatGPT.

No advanced AI experience is required; follow along or watch and try later.
