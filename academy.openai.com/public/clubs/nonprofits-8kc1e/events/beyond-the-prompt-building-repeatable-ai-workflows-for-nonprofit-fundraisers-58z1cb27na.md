# ChatGPT for Work

#### OpenAI Academy is joining CauseVox’s Nonprofit Fundraising Summit: Impact Overflow for a practical session on building repeatable, human-centered AI workflows for fundraising.

Using a year-end campaign as an example, Alex Nawar, Head of OpenAI Academy, will demonstrate how ChatGPT Work can help teams review source materials, develop a campaign plan, turn a proven process into a reusable Skill, and prepare recurring campaign updates. The goal is to spend less time on repeatable work while keeping human judgment, empathy, trust, and donor relationships at the center.

Impact Overflow is a free, virtual gathering for nonprofit professionals produced by CauseVox on September 30 and October 1.

### What to expect

* Identify fundraising work that is repeatable, stable, and easy to verify

* Plan a multistep workflow using trusted files, clear goals, and human approval points

* See how a year-end campaign process can become a reusable Skill or recurring update

* Explore an approach that can also support grants, donor stewardship, and fundraising operations

### Who should attend

Fundraising, development, communications, and nonprofit operations professionals who want to move beyond one-off AI prompts and build workflows their teams can reuse.
