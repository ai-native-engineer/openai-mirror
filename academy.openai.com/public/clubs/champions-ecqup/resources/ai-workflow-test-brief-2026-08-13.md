## Get alignment and approval on a controlled test of an AI workflow.

August 13, 2026

![AI workflow test brief](https://cdn.gradual.com/images/https://d2xo500swnpgl1.cloudfront.net/uploads/oaiacademy/ai-workflow-test-brief-style-thumb-a76de97a-b48d-47ec-93db-ea8ba181ceb4-1786663928027.jpeg?fit=scale-down&width=1200)

**Safe-to-Test AI Workflow Approval Brief**

*A one-page template for agreeing on a controlled AI workflow test.*

Use this brief to agree on one controlled test. Keep each answer to one or two lines. If something is unknown, name who will resolve it and by when.

## **1. Decision requested**

**Sponsor:** [name]

**Decision needed by:** [date]

**Ask:** Approve / revise / defer a [duration] test with [cohort].

**Support needed:** [access, funding, or leader reinforcement]

﻿

## **2. Workflow and intended result**

**Workflow:** [specific task]

**Current owner:** [person or team]

**Business priority:** [priority]

**Expected result beyond time saved:** [quality, consistency, speed to decision, customer outcome, etc.]

**Success signals:** [one or two observable measures]

﻿

## **3. Test boundary**

**Users and dates:** [named group; start–end]

**Approved inputs:** [systems, folders, or data]

**Excluded data:** [data the workflow must not use]

**May read:** [sources]

**May write:** [draft or live destinations]

**May share or publish:** [audience and channel]

**Out of scope:** [actions the test will not take]

﻿

## **4. Controls and review**

**Access restrictions:** [workspace, role/group, sign-in, or connector limits]

**Retention or deletion:** [requirement]

**Human approval required before:** [data write, publication, external delivery, or other consequential action]

**Reviewer:** [name]

**Escalation owner:** [name]

**Test record:** [where to keep source links, versions, approvals, changes, errors, and overrides]

﻿

## **5. Test plan and rules**

**Workflow owner:** [name]

**IT/security partner:** [name, if needed]

**Instructions and support:** [link or channel]

**Continue when:** [success and control conditions]

**Pause and investigate when:** [recoverable concern]

**Stop when:** [material risk or failure]

Only [role] may restart the test.

﻿

## **6. Evidence and next decision**

**Collect:** [outcome signal], [quality or correction rate], [control issues], [user feedback], and [support effort].

**Evidence owner:** [name]

**Scale decision date:** [date]

At the end, [sponsor] will expand, revise, or stop the workflow based on this evidence.

## **Recommendation**

Test [workflow] with [cohort] for [duration] to improve [business outcome]. Use only [approved inputs], require human approval before [consequential action], and stop if [condition].

## **Final check**

1. The outcome is specific.

2. Read/write/share limits are clear.

3. Human review covers the risky step.

4. Continue/pause/stop rules are observable.

5. One person owns the next decision.

﻿
