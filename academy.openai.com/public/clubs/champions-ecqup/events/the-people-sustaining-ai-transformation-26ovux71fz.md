# The People Sustaining AI Transformation

# Deployment & Adoption

# Champions

As AI adoption spreads across an organization, the work gets more complex than any one Champion can handle. Building a useful workflow, getting leaders to invest in it, and supporting the teams that rely on it take different people working together.

Join Christina Meng, Champion Programs Lead at OpenAI, to hear what we learned from Q3 conversations with OpenAI Champion Network members about the teams behind sustained AI transformation.

**What you’ll take away:**

* Our new Champion Role Guide introduces four roles: Executive Sponsors, Transformation Leaders, Agent Activators, and Admins. We’ll share what each does and use an example to show how they work together

* Where people need dedicated time, better information, or the authority to make a decision, and who can provide that support

* What to look for beyond job titles, from the colleague others turn to for help using AI to the leader who can commit resources when a workflow is worth expanding
