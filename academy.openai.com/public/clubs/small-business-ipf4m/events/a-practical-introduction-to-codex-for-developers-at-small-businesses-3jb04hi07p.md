# A practical introduction to Codex for developers at small businesses

Join OpenAI for a practical look at how engineering teams at small businesses and agencies can use Codex in their development workflows — from understanding an unfamiliar codebase to shipping tested, review-ready changes.

In this session, we’ll focus on practical entry points that don’t require a large engineering organization or a major workflow overhaul. You’ll see how a lean engineering team can start with one well-scoped task, give Codex the right context and constraints, and build toward more repeatable development workflows while keeping engineers in control of what ships.

Hear from **Matt Smidebush, Head of SMB at OpenAI,** and **Sophia Qin, Solutions Engineer,** as they share how small businesses can make Codex part of your development team.

**In this pre-recorded session, we’ll cover:**

* How to give Codex the context it needs to understand an existing codebase

* How to turn an issue, feature request, or customer need into a scoped plan and working implementation

* How to use Codex for code review, debugging, refactoring, and ongoing maintenance

* How to turn your team’s coding standards and workflows into lightweight instructions with files such as AGENTS.md

* How to keep engineers in control with a review-first workflow for validating changes, tests, and what ultimately ships
