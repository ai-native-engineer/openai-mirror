# ChatGPT for small businesses - Houston, TX

# Workplace & Business

# **Prompt better. Research smarter. Build something useful.**

Join OpenAI Academy and SCORE for a hands-on workshop for Houston-area small-business owners, operators, founders, and team leads. You’ll watch practical demos, try guided prompts on a real business task, and leave with one reusable workflow or asset you can use after the session.

No technical background is required. Bring a laptop or tablet, arrive able to sign in to ChatGPT, and use only information you are comfortable sharing.

What you’ll do

* Turn a rough request into a clearer prompt

* Research a business question and check sources

* Turn the result into a useful communication, plan, or workflow

* Build, review, and save one method you can reuse

# Schedule

* 9:30 AM — Registration and breakfast

* 10:00 AM — Prompt better + research smarter

* 11:15 AM — Break

* 11:30 AM — Build something useful

* 1:00 PM — Workshop ends

Eligible attendees will receive 3 months of ChatGPT Plus after the workshop. Activation details will be shared at the event.
