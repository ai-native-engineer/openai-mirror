# ChatGPT for small business: Santa Barbara, CA

Register

Build your AI skills and learn directly from OpenAI Academy how ChatGPT can support the work you do every day—from researching an idea to drafting a customer message or organizing a recurring task.

OpenAI Academy, the Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development (GO-Biz), and the Economic Development Collaborative (EDC) invite Santa Barbara–area business leaders, small-business owners, operators, and employees to a hands-on professional development workshop.

Set aside a morning to explore what ChatGPT can do, try it on a task that matters to your work, and ask questions along the way. Come on your own or with colleagues.

### What you’ll learn

• Write clearer prompts, set up ChatGPT for your needs, and use it responsibly.
• Explore research and communication tasks while checking sources and reviewing the results.
• Practice using files and tools to build a repeatable workflow for your business.

### What to expect

The workshop combines demonstrations, individual practice, sharing, and Q&A. Mentors will be available to support your learning and help with questions as you work through the activities.

Bring a laptop, your questions, and a nonconfidential business task you would like to explore. You’ll have time to test ideas and consider where ChatGPT could fit into your work.

### Optional ChatGPT Plus offer

Eligible attendees will have the option to claim three months of ChatGPT Plus at no cost.
