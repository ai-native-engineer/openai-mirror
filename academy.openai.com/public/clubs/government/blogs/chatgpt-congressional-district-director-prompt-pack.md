## 30 practical prompts for district and state directors. Copy a prompt, add your details, and get started.

This pack gives District and State Directors 30 ready-to-use ChatGPT prompts for everyday congressional office work.

Use it to:
• Prepare for a Member visit.
• Write a short update for the Washington office.
• Organize community feedback and follow-up tasks.

Get started
Open the prompt pack below and choose a task. Copy a prompt into your office-approved ChatGPT workspace, replace the bracketed placeholders, and add the information the prompt asks for. Review the draft, then ask for any changes you need.

Use only information your office permits in that workspace. Keep individual case details separate from general office updates. Follow your office rules and keep official work separate from campaign activity.
