## 30 practical prompts for legislative directors. Copy a prompt, add your details, and get started.

This pack gives Legislative Directors 30 ready-to-use ChatGPT prompts for everyday congressional office work.

Use it to:
• Plan the legislative team’s agenda.
• Review briefing drafts for missing facts and unclear points.
• Compare legislative updates with your office tracker.

Get started
Open the prompt pack below and choose a task. Copy a prompt into your office-approved ChatGPT workspace, replace the bracketed placeholders, and add the information the prompt asks for. Review the draft, then ask for any changes you need.

Use only information your office permits in that workspace. Verify bill versions and legislative status before using a draft. Follow your office rules and keep official work separate from campaign activity.
