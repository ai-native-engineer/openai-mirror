## 30 practical prompts for legislative correspondents. Copy a prompt, add your details, and get started.

This pack gives Legislative Correspondents 30 ready-to-use ChatGPT prompts for everyday congressional office work.

Use it to:
• Identify the questions in a constituent letter.
• Draft a reply using approved office language.
• Update response templates and make them easier to read.

Get started
Open the prompt pack below and choose a task. Copy a prompt into your office-approved ChatGPT workspace, replace the bracketed placeholders, and add the information the prompt asks for. Review the draft, then ask for any changes you need.

Use only information your office permits in that workspace. Check that each reply reflects the Member’s approved position and current facts. Follow your office rules and keep official work separate from campaign activity.
