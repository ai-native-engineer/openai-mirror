## 30 practical prompts for communications directors and press secretaries. Copy a prompt, add your details, and get started.

![ChatGPT Prompt Pack for Congressional Communications Staff](https://cdn.gradual.com/images/https://d2xo500swnpgl1.cloudfront.net/uploads/oaiacademy/cover-06-0c278d8c-ddd8-457a-b534-4e321065de3e-1789998588750.jpeg?fit=scale-down&width=1200)

This pack gives Communications Directors and Press Secretaries 30 ready-to-use ChatGPT prompts for everyday congressional office work.

Use it to:
• Draft a release from approved facts.
• Prepare factual questions and answers for media interviews.
• Check that releases, newsletters, and social posts agree.

Get started
Open the prompt pack below and choose a task. Copy a prompt into your office-approved ChatGPT workspace, replace the bracketed placeholders, and add the information the prompt asks for. Review the draft, then ask for any changes you need.

Use only information your office permits in that workspace. Verify quotations and factual claims, and follow your office release process. Follow your office rules and keep official work separate from campaign activity.
