## 30 practical prompts for staff assistants. Copy a prompt, add your details, and get started.

This pack gives Staff Assistants 30 ready-to-use ChatGPT prompts for everyday congressional office work.

Use it to:
• Practice phone greetings and responses.
• Sort requests using your office routing guide.
• Prepare visitor information and shift handoffs.

Get started
Open the prompt pack below and choose a task. Copy a prompt into your office-approved ChatGPT workspace, replace the bracketed placeholders, and add the information the prompt asks for. Review the draft, then ask for any changes you need.

Use only information your office permits in that workspace. Check that drafts do not promise an unconfirmed meeting, tour, or response. Follow your office rules and keep official work separate from campaign activity.
