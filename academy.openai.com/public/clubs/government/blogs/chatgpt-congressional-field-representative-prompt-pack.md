## 30 practical prompts for field representatives and outreach coordinators. Copy a prompt, add your details, and get started.

![ChatGPT Prompt Pack for Congressional Field Representatives](https://cdn.gradual.com/images/https://d2xo500swnpgl1.cloudfront.net/uploads/oaiacademy/cover-14-817f23e4-b3ae-4c04-b8c9-3a1a05175de2-1789998916682.jpeg?fit=scale-down&width=1200)

This pack gives Field Representatives and Outreach Coordinators 30 ready-to-use ChatGPT prompts for everyday congressional office work.

Use it to:
• Prepare questions for a community meeting.
• Turn meeting notes into a short report.
• Track requests, commitments, and next steps.

Get started
Open the prompt pack below and choose a task. Copy a prompt into your office-approved ChatGPT workspace, replace the bracketed placeholders, and add the information the prompt asks for. Review the draft, then ask for any changes you need.

Use only information your office permits in that workspace. Keep personal case details separate from general meeting reports. Follow your office rules and keep official work separate from campaign activity.
