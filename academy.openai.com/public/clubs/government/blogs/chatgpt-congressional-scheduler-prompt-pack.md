## 30 practical prompts for schedulers and directors of scheduling. Copy a prompt, add your details, and get started.

![ChatGPT Prompt Pack for Congressional Schedulers](https://cdn.gradual.com/images/https://d2xo500swnpgl1.cloudfront.net/uploads/oaiacademy/cover-08-040c7119-b445-4edd-adbf-8075b499776f-1789998658990.jpeg?fit=scale-down&width=1200)

This pack gives Schedulers and Directors of Scheduling 30 ready-to-use ChatGPT prompts for everyday congressional office work.

Use it to:
• Summarize incoming meeting requests.
• Check itineraries for conflicting details.
• Prepare a clear end-of-day handoff.

Get started
Open the prompt pack below and choose a task. Copy a prompt into your office-approved ChatGPT workspace, replace the bracketed placeholders, and add the information the prompt asks for. Review the draft, then ask for any changes you need.

Use only information your office permits in that workspace. Verify times, locations, and confirmations. A draft does not change the calendar or notify attendees. Follow your office rules and keep official work separate from campaign activity.
