## 30 practical prompts for constituent services directors. Copy a prompt, add your details, and get started.

This pack gives Constituent Services Directors 30 ready-to-use ChatGPT prompts for everyday congressional office work.

Use it to:
• Create clear case-note and handoff guidelines.
• Prepare questions for an agency liaison.
• Summarize team activity for office leadership.

Get started
Open the prompt pack below and choose a task. Copy a prompt into your office-approved ChatGPT workspace, replace the bracketed placeholders, and add the information the prompt asks for. Review the draft, then ask for any changes you need.

Use only information your office permits in that workspace. Use de-identified examples for practice. Staff must make decisions about service and case handling. Follow your office rules and keep official work separate from campaign activity.
