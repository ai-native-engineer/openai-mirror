## 30 practical prompts for office managers and administrative directors. Copy a prompt, add your details, and get started.

![ChatGPT Prompt Pack for Congressional Office Managers](https://cdn.gradual.com/images/https://d2xo500swnpgl1.cloudfront.net/uploads/oaiacademy/cover-09-b8c9778f-f960-49d6-b5cb-8491c047a1ca-1789998722444.jpeg?fit=scale-down&width=1200)

This pack gives Office Managers and Administrative Directors 30 ready-to-use ChatGPT prompts for everyday congressional office work.

Use it to:
• Turn office procedures into simple checklists.
• Prepare new-staff onboarding instructions.
• Draft purchase requests and weekly operations updates.

Get started
Open the prompt pack below and choose a task. Copy a prompt into your office-approved ChatGPT workspace, replace the bracketed placeholders, and add the information the prompt asks for. Review the draft, then ask for any changes you need.

Use only information your office permits in that workspace. Keep required approvals in place and confirm procedures with the responsible staff. Follow your office rules and keep official work separate from campaign activity.
