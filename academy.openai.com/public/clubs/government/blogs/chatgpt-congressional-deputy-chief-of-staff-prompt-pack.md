## 30 practical prompts for deputy chiefs of staff. Copy a prompt, add your details, and get started.

![Laura  Keenan](https://cdn.gradual.com/images/https://d2xo500swnpgl1.cloudfront.net/uploads/oaiacademy/Headshot-53798635-8187-41c2-bd0a-15cee49c1e98-1784555485528.jpeg?fit=scale-down&width=60)

![ChatGPT Prompt Pack for Deputy Chiefs of Staff](https://cdn.gradual.com/images/https://d2xo500swnpgl1.cloudfront.net/uploads/oaiacademy/cover-02-d30494ee-2bfa-4891-9dc4-0f5324d71f88-1789997945809.jpeg?fit=scale-down&width=1200)

This pack gives Deputy Chiefs of Staff 30 ready-to-use ChatGPT prompts for everyday congressional office work.

Use it to:
• Clarify assignments and who owns them.
• Prepare agendas for cross-team meetings.
• Turn project updates into a short leadership summary.

Get started
Open the prompt pack below and choose a task. Copy a prompt into your office-approved ChatGPT workspace, replace the bracketed placeholders, and add the information the prompt asks for. Review the draft, then ask for any changes you need.

Use only information your office permits in that workspace. Confirm responsibilities and deadlines with the team. Keep proposed plans distinct from approved ones. Follow your office rules and keep official work separate from campaign activity.
