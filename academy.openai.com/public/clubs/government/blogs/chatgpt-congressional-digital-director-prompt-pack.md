## 30 practical prompts for digital directors and digital assistants. Copy a prompt, add your details, and get started.

![ChatGPT Prompt Pack for Congressional Digital Staff](https://cdn.gradual.com/images/https://d2xo500swnpgl1.cloudfront.net/uploads/oaiacademy/cover-07-675a472c-e7f5-457e-96b7-3cea3bc759a1-1789998623962.jpeg?fit=scale-down&width=1200)

This pack gives Digital Directors and Digital Assistants 30 ready-to-use ChatGPT prompts for everyday congressional office work.

Use it to:
• Make website instructions easier to follow.
• Turn approved information into social posts or short video scripts.
• Check facts across drafts before publishing.

Get started
Open the prompt pack below and choose a task. Copy a prompt into your office-approved ChatGPT workspace, replace the bracketed placeholders, and add the information the prompt asks for. Review the draft, then ask for any changes you need.

Use only information your office permits in that workspace. Check links, dates, and accessibility text before publishing. Follow your office rules and keep official work separate from campaign activity.
