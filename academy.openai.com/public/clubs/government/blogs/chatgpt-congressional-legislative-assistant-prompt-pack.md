## 30 practical prompts for legislative assistants and legislative aides. Copy a prompt, add your details, and get started.

This pack gives Legislative Assistants and Legislative Aides 30 ready-to-use ChatGPT prompts for everyday congressional office work.

Use it to:
• Turn source material into a short issue brief.
• Draft hearing questions from supplied testimony.
• Prepare meeting briefs and guidance for constituent replies.

Get started
Open the prompt pack below and choose a task. Copy a prompt into your office-approved ChatGPT workspace, replace the bracketed placeholders, and add the information the prompt asks for. Review the draft, then ask for any changes you need.

Use only information your office permits in that workspace. Check source dates and distinguish verified facts from stakeholder claims. Follow your office rules and keep official work separate from campaign activity.
