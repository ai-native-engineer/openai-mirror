## ChatGPT Work helps News UK Broadcasting accelerate commercial analysis, stress-test financial assumptions, identify risks, and sharpen executive decision-making.

![How News UK Broadcasting Uses ChatGPT Work as a Strategic Finance Partner](https://cdn.gradual.com/images/https://d2xo500swnpgl1.cloudfront.net/uploads/oaiacademy/Screenshot-2026-08-06-at-18-24-cb299910-2ad2-40b9-95bf-267b947a3336-1786037092050.jpeg?fit=scale-down&width=1200)

In the fast-paced environment of News UK Broadcasting (home to brands like talkSPORT, Times Radio, and Virgin Radio) commercial finance teams are constantly evaluating complex business models, evaluating partner proposals, and presenting strategic recommendations to executive leadership.

To accelerate analysis and enhance strategic decision-making, finance leadership introduced ChatGPT Enterprise. Here is how one commercial finance leader integrated Enterprise AI into their daily workflow to act as a thought partner, risk assessor, and executive communication editor.

### **The Challenge**

Navigating commercial proposals, licensing terms, and commercial models requires balancing complex economics, audience assumptions, rights constraints, and contract risks. Traditional workflows often involve navigating ambiguous questions and sifting through dense legal or financial documents from scratch, a process that can slow down strategic momentum.

The finance team needed a tool that could:

* Accelerate the movement from broad, ambiguous business questions to structured analytical frameworks.

* Offer rigorous "sparring" to stress-test financial assumptions, identify hidden risks, and explore alternative commercial models.

* Work safely within an approved, enterprise-grade environment to handle sensitive internal business context.

### **The Solution: An AI Strategic "Sparring Partner"**

Using ChatGPT Enterprise, News UK Broadcasting’s commercial finance team established an AI-driven workflow focused on commercial negotiation, business case development, risk review, and senior stakeholder communication.

#### **Key Use Cases**

1. **Commercial Proposal & Negotiation Support**

* *Scenario:* Interrogating a proposal from a partner regarding a new distribution channel.

* *How ChatGPT Helped:* ChatGPT Work was used to unpack the underlying economics behind headline revenue shares. It identified commercial risks and helped draft focused negotiation questions around revenue expectations, content rights, cost structures, reporting standards, exclusivity terms, and potential financial guarantees. The tool identified that the proposed commercial terms with complicated caveats actually equated to 30% less than originally advertised, allowing for quick ongoing negotiation.

2. **Strategic Business Case Development**

* *Scenario:* Developing new commercial models for key brands within News Broadcasting, alongside considering brand new audience model proposals.

* *How AI Helped:* Functioning as a sparring partner, ChatGPT Work stress-tested audience assumptions, commercial economics, potential revenue cannibalisation, rights constraints, and incentive structures, allowing the team to refine multiple commercial options before presenting to senior stakeholders.

3. **Contract & Commercial Risk Review**

* *Scenario:* Rapidly reviewing supplier agreements from a commercial finance perspective.

* *How AI Helped:* ChatGPT Work distilled lengthy legal agreements into concise recommendations, flagging payment mechanics, revenue share terms, cash flow impacts, audit rights, and key commercial risks. This resulted in targeted contract amendments in a fraction of the usual time. Reduced first-pass strategic review by around 60-70% whilst allowing for deeper interrogation instantly.

4. **Executive-Level Communication**

* *Scenario:* Refining investor-relations presentations and deep-dive analyses for executive leadership.

* *How AI Helped:* The tool sharpened core narratives, highlighted high-impact metrics, anticipated potential executive board questions, and polished presentation materials into board-ready deliverables.

### **Practical Governance & Human Control**

* **Data Security:** Operating within the approved Enterprise environment ensures that internal business context and sensitive financial documents remain strictly protected and confidential.

### **Key Impact & Results**

* **Faster Strategic Velocity:** Reduced first-pass strategic review by around 60-70% whilst allowing for deeper interrogation instantly.

* **Richer Risk Identification:** Improved the ability to stress-test assumptions, spot overlooked risks, and map out alternative commercial avenues prior to negotiation. Quickly identified that the proposed commercial terms with complicated caveats actually equated to 30% less than originally advertised, allowing for quick ongoing negotiation.

* **Polished Executive Materials:** Enhanced the clarity, narrative quality, and preparedness of materials submitted to senior stakeholders and board members.

* **Workflow Integration:** Transformed day-to-day finance operations by turning AI into an indispensable tool for commercial strategy and decision support.
