# Intro to ChatGPT Work for News Organizations

# News Organizations

# News

How can ChatGPT help move the business of news forward?

Join the OpenAI Academy for News Organizations for an introductory session on how ChatGPT Work can support the teams that help news organizations grow and operate. We’ll explore the shift from AI that answers questions to AI that can carry work forward across multiple steps, using the tools and context around you.

You’ll see a few practical examples of how agents, skills, and plugins can support research, analysis, planning, and other recurring workflows. You’ll also learn ways ChatGPT Work can create space to test new ideas, share what works with your teams, and build the capacity to take on higher-value work.

Tune in for live demonstrations, practical ideas, and a closer look at what becomes possible when ChatGPT works alongside your team.
