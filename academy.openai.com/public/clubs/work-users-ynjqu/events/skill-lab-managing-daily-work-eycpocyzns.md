# Portfolio Company Knowledge Work

Daily work can involve a lot of small decisions: what to prepare for, what to answer, what to follow up on, and what can wait. In this hands-on lab, you will practice building a daily workflow that helps you move through that work with more focus and less context switching.

We will show how to gather the right inputs, summarize what matters, prepare for meetings, prioritize tasks, draft responses, and track follow-ups in a way that stays reviewable. The goal is not to hand off judgment, but to create a repeatable structure for getting oriented and moving the day forward.

You will leave with a daily work playbook you can adapt for meeting prep, inbox triage, task prioritization, and follow-up tracking.
