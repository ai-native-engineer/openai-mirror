# ChatGPT Edu 102 for K-12

Move beyond the basics and explore how ChatGPT Edu can support more complex work across teaching, professional learning, school leadership, and district operations. This session builds on the concepts introduced in ChatGPT Edu 101 and demonstrates how educators and staff can improve results through stronger prompting, iterative refinement, approved files, and multistep workflows.

The OpenAI Education team will also introduce agentic capabilities, including ChatGPT Work and Workspace Agents where enabled. Participants will see practical examples of how these tools can help organize research, prepare communications, support recurring processes, and develop useful work products while maintaining appropriate human oversight and district-specific safeguards.

In this session, you’ll learn how to:

• Strengthen results with follow-up prompts, clearer instructions, and iterative review.

• Use approved documents and source materials to support more grounded responses.

• Organize larger projects into structured steps and reusable workflows.

• Explore how ChatGPT Work can support research, planning, and finished deliverables.

• Understand how Workspace Agents can assist with recurring, reviewable tasks.

• Maintain appropriate permissions, human approval, and responsible-use practices.

Attendance at ChatGPT Edu 101 or equivalent familiarity with ChatGPT is recommended. Specific agentic features and connected tools may vary depending on district workspace settings and administrator permissions.

This session repeats the advanced material from the August 20 ChatGPT Edu 102 offering. Participants only need to attend one 102 session. A recording will be available for those who cannot attend live.
