## CONTENT & TRANSCRIPT

Meet Dots, personal AI assistants in ChatGPT that can take on ongoing tasks and help you stay organized throughout the day.

In this quick demo, see how a fifth-grade teacher uses a Dot to receive daily briefings, prepare for parent-teacher night, keep track of schedule changes, and get proactive reminders.

U.S. K-12 educators can verify their eligibility for free access to ChatGPT for Teachers at  <https://chatgpt.com/k12-verification>﻿
