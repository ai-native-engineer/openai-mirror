## CONTENT & TRANSCRIPT

Dr. Dario Rodriguez, Ed.D., MSF, is a finance professor at Miami Dade College and Westcliff University, specializing in blending traditional financial education with emerging technologies. He leverages AI, Python, and data visualization tools to create engaging, real-world learning experiences that prepare students for the modern financial industry. Bilingual in English and Spanish, he is passionate about advancing inclusive, technology-driven education.
