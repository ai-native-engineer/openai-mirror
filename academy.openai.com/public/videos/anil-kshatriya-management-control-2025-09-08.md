## SUMMARY

Demonstrates how AI-enhanced preparation improves the quality, confidence, and engagement of student Balanced Scorecard projects in a Management Control course. Highlights a case-based approach where ChatGPT supports brainstorming, stress-testing assumptions, and enriching peer discussions.

## CONTENT & TRANSCRIPT

Anil is a professor of Management Accounting and Control at ESSEC Business School, France, teaching in the Master in Management program. His research and teaching focus on how incentives, information, and organizational design shape managerial decision-making. He integrates case-based learning and AI tools to bridge theory and practice in the classroom.
