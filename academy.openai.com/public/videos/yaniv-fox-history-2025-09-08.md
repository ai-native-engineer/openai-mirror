## SUMMARY

In this video, Yaniv Fox shows how he uses custom GPTs to help students prepare for exams, how he uses projects to automate lesson plan compositions, and how he uses Advanced Voice Mode to prepare for lectures.

## CONTENT & TRANSCRIPT

Yaniv Fox is a professor of late antiquity and the early middle ages at Bar-Ilan University, specializing in Merovingian history. He is also vice dean of the faculty of humanities and head of AI initiatives at Bar-Ilan, and is writing a book titled: Using AI in History for Cambridge University Press.
