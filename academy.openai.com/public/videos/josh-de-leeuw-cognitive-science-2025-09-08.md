# Josh de Leeuw (Cognitive Science)

Posted Aug 01, 2025 | Views 174

Josh de Leeuw describes an assignment where students engage in a conversation with a custom GPT to demonstrate and deepen their understanding of philosophical ideas in his course.

Josh's research is focused on the development and application of research software for the study of human cognition. He created the widely-used jsPsych experiment software for conducting behavioral experiments online. He uses these tools to study how people learn and represent new information.
