# Have a business idea you’d like to explore?

Whether you’re considering a side business, self-employment, or a new direction for an existing business, getting started means figuring out who you want to serve and what you still need to learn. In this 30-minute session, we’ll explore how ChatGPT can help you turn an early idea into a starting plan.

Through guided examples, we’ll explore how to:
• Describe a potential customer and the problem your idea could help solve.
• Organize your idea into a simple outline, separating what you know from assumptions you need to test.
• Develop questions for a customer conversation and identify a first step to explore your idea.

You’ll leave with a starting outline and prompts you can adapt to your own idea. No prior AI experience is required.

Presented by OpenAI Academy in collaboration with Veterans Forge.
