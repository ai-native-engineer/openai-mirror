# ChatGPT for veterans - Nashville, TN

[Register](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdU49FAyy_mDr-H_bBW5BXJBIM3wFKkSVeX4mOV4kxCxlnrdA/viewform)

Join OpenAI Academy, Veterans Forge, and Wounded Warrior Project for a hands-on workshop helping veterans and transitioning service members build confidence with AI. Explore tools for career development, writing, research, and everyday productivity through guided instruction and exercises.

We’ll begin together with opening remarks and a brief orientation. Participants will then divide into two learning tracks based on experience, with demonstrations, real-world exercises, and support as they try the tools themselves.

Build reusable prompts and repeatable workflows, and explore ideas for applying AI to your next chapter. We’ll close with key takeaways and questions, followed by an optional lunch and networking.

Who should attend: Veterans and transitioning service members who want hands-on experience with AI.

No advanced technical background is required. Advance registration is required. Lunch is provided.

Eligible participants will receive three months of ChatGPT Plus, with activation details shared upon attendance.
