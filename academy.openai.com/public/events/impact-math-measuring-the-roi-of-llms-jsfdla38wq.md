# Impact Math: Measuring the ROI of LLMs

Register

How do you know whether an AI pilot is delivering meaningful value? Join David Sperry for a practical session on measuring the return on investment of large language models in government workflows. We’ll explore how to establish a baseline, measure changes in time and quality, and account for the effort involved in adoption and human review. Learn how to turn those findings into a clear, credible case for what to scale, refine, or stop.
