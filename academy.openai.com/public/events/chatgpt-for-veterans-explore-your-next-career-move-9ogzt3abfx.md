# ChatGPT for veterans: Your experience. Your next move.

Register

# Thinking about your first civilian job, a career change, or a new opportunity?

Your experience gives you a starting point, but finding the words to explain it to a new employer can be challenging. In this 30-minute session, we’ll explore how ChatGPT can help you identify relevant skills, describe your experience clearly, and prepare for career conversations.

Through guided examples, we’ll explore how to:
• Translate military, civilian, or volunteer responsibilities into skills an employer can recognize.
• Turn an experience into a clear resume bullet, without adding achievements or qualifications you haven’t earned.
• Practice answering interview questions using examples from your own experience.

You’ll leave with prompts you can adapt to your goals and a clearer sense of how to review and refine ChatGPT’s suggestions. No prior AI experience is required.

Presented by OpenAI Academy in collaboration with Veterans Forge.
