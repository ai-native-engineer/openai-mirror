# ChatGPT for veterans : Make sense of VA benefits

# Looking for a clearer way to approach VA benefits information?

Knowing where to start and which sources to trust can make benefits research feel overwhelming. In this 30-minute session, we’ll explore how ChatGPT can help you work through public VA guidance and prepare for a more informed conversation with VA or an accredited representative.

Through guided examples, we’ll explore how to:
• Turn a broad benefits question into focused research questions.
• Ask for plain-language explanations and check responses against official VA sources.
• Organize what you’ve learned, flag what still needs verification, and prepare questions for qualified support.

You’ll leave with a reusable research checklist and prompts to support your next steps. No prior AI experience is required.

This session does not determine eligibility or prepare or submit claims. Please keep personal claim, medical, and identifying information out of the activities and event chat.

Presented by OpenAI Academy in collaboration with Veterans Forge.
