[Register](https://forms.gle/AvVoWCWME3YSbjux9)

# Use ChatGPT to move one real thing forward.

Whether you’re looking for your next job, thinking about school, starting a business, or just trying to make everyday tasks easier, this workshop is built to help you take a practical next step.

Join OpenAI Academy and NPower for a hands-on session for veterans, military spouses, and military-connected community members. You’ll watch practical examples, try ChatGPT on a task that matters to you, and leave with one thing improved and a clearer sense of how AI can support whatever comes next.

No prior AI experience is required.

How it works
• Watch a practical example
• Try it on a real task
• Share what worked and leave with a next step

What you’ll do
• Write clearer prompts that produce more useful results
• Research and organize information more effectively
• Turn a rough idea or task into a useful draft, plan, or workflow
• Review what worked and identify a next step you can reuse

What to bring
Bring a laptop or tablet, a ChatGPT account, and one task you’ve been meaning to improve.

Eligible attendees will receive three free months of ChatGPT Plus. Activation details will be shared during the session.
