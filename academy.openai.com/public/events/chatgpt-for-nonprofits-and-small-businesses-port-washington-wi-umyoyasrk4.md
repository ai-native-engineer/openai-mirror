# ChatGPT for nonprofits and small businesses: Port Washington, WI

# A morning workshop for local teams across Ozaukee County.

Join OpenAI Academy and the Ozaukee Nonprofit Center for a free, hands-on morning designed for nonprofit and small business staff and leaders in Port Washington and across Ozaukee County. Explore how ChatGPT can support your work: developing ideas, drafting communications, researching questions, and turning recurring tasks into more manageable workflows.

Through guided demonstrations and time to try things yourself, you’ll practice giving ChatGPT clearer instructions, refining its responses, and checking its work. Come on your own or bring colleagues so you can learn together and discuss where these skills could help your organization.

What you’ll explore
• Writing and refining prompts for everyday nonprofit work
• Brainstorming, drafting, and researching with ChatGPT
• Building repeatable approaches to recurring tasks
• Reviewing outputs thoughtfully and protecting sensitive information
