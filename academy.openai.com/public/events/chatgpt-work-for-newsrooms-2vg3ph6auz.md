How can your newsroom level up when it comes to AI?

OpenAI Academy is hosting ChatGPT Work for Newsrooms, a virtual session on moving from one-off prompts to practical tools built around the work journalists already do.

See how reporters and editors can use ChatGPT Work to research stories, analyze documents and data, prepare interviews, develop coverage plans, and turn dense source material into clear, sourced briefs. We’ll demonstrate how agents, skills, and custom apps can transform a useful workflow into something an entire newsroom can reuse.

Tune in for live examples, practical ideas, and a closer look at what becomes possible when ChatGPT works the way your newsroom does.
