# Skill Labs

Some work is easier to use when it becomes a shared page, lightweight site, or dashboard instead of another document or folder of links. In this hands-on lab, you will practice turning source materials into a simple experience that helps people find information, understand status, and take the next step.

We will start with a practical use case, define the audience, organize source links, choose the most useful status cards or filters, and create clear guidance for keeping the experience accurate over time. You will also learn what to review before sharing, including source visibility, access, freshness, and ownership.

You will leave with a site or dashboard brief that can guide a first build and help your team maintain it after launch.
