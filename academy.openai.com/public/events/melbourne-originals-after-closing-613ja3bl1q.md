# Spend less time on tasks, more time on your craft.

OpenAI is bringing local business owners and operators together for a hands-on workshop exploring how ChatGPT can help with the everyday tasks of running a business, making more time for customers and craft.

From the market stalls along the Esplanade to the cafés, restaurants, boutiques and studios that give our neighbourhoods their character, small businesses are part of the cultural fabric of Melbourne. Behind every independent label, design studio, neighbourhood shop or café is work that carries on after closing: answering enquiries, preparing proposals, coordinating suppliers and planning what comes next.

Join us for an evening of practical learning and conversation with other local businesses in the lead-up to the busy Christmas trading period. Whether you’re new to ChatGPT or already using it, you’ll leave with ideas and skills you can put to work in your business.

Please bring your laptop so you can try ChatGPT on a task from your own business during the workshop.
