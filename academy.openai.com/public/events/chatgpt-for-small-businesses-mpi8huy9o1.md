# ChatGPT for small businesses

*This event is by invitation only.*

Join us for an interactive, hands-on event presented in partnership with the Brooklyn Chamber of Commerce, with support from Caribbean Business Network (Caribbiz Network) and Law Social House. Designed to help small business owners turn everyday challenges into practical solutions with ChatGPT, the session will give you actionable skills to save time, streamline your work, and bring new ideas to life. You’ll leave with a solution you can begin using in your business right away. Dinner will be provided.

During the session, we’ll cover:

1. Practical ways to use ChatGPT and improve your prompts
2. How to build repeatable workflows with ChatGPT Work and Projects
3. GPTs, plugins, and other tools that can support your business
4. Hands-on time to create a solution tailored to your need

Please bring a fully charged laptop or tablet so you can participate in the session’s hands-on activities. All attendees will receive upgraded access to ChatGPT as part of their participation in the event.
