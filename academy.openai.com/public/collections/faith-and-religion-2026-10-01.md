### A software engineer builds a Muslim prayer app with ChatGPT and Codex

A near-fatal car crash led Kamran Mohammed and his wife to reconnect with Islam. Frustrated by worship apps crowded with ads or difficult for beginners, the Austin software engineer used ChatGPT and Codex to build My Amaal. The free, ad-free app offers illustrated prayer instructions and lets users track their prayers without sending their worship history to a company server.
