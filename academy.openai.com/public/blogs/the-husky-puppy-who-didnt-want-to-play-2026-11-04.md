## Mario Quintero’s daily notes in ChatGPT helped him decide when Oreo needed a vet.

![The husky puppy who didn’t want to play](https://cdn.gradual.com/images/https://d2xo500swnpgl1.cloudfront.net/uploads/oaiacademy/Oreo-and-Mario-517f8d7f-407a-45ca-8e00-ea11748d2f87-1793816825229.jpeg?fit=scale-down&width=1200)

Mexico City digital marketer  [Mario Quintero](https://www.linkedin.com/in/quinteromario/?locale=en) kept a journal in ChatGPT about his new husky puppy, Oreo. When Oreo became ill, those notes helped Quintero decide when to get him to a veterinarian. Quintero grew up with dogs in his parents’ house in Cuernavaca, but after years without a pet, adopting again felt like a major step. He and his wife had long pictured bringing home a husky. In July 2025, they found Oreo through a Facebook adoption group. He was about two months old.

Quintero wanted to make sure he did everything right, so he observed Oreo closely and noted everything in a journal, feeding his observations to ChatGPT. Day by day, he logged what he saw and asked questions about what he should expect from a husky puppy adjusting to a new home. Oreo weighed about 5 pounds when they adopted him. Quintero expected high energy, curiosity, and constant play. Instead, the puppy seemed unusually calm. He did not appear hungry. He did not want to play. Even accounting for the stress of a new environment, that pattern felt wrong.

As Quintero continued describing symptoms to ChatGPT, its responses became more urgent. When Oreo developed diarrhea that wouldn’t go away, ChatGPT flagged it as abnormal and prompted Quintero to take him to a veterinarian. Within a week of being adopted, Oreo was at the vet and hospitalized. The veterinarian diagnosed him with parvovirus, a highly contagious disease that can spread through exposure to infected feces and contaminated environments, and which is especially dangerous for unvaccinated puppies.

Parvovirus attacks the intestines and can cause severe vomiting, diarrhea and dehydration. It can be fatal, especially without prompt veterinary care. Oreo had not yet been vaccinated when the couple adopted him.

Oreo spent four days in the hospital receiving supportive care, Quintero says. When Oreo came home, his personality snapped into focus. He was suddenly the dog Quintero had expected from day one: energized, loud, playful, always ready to run, explore, and get into everything. By February 2026, Quintero was still using ChatGPT to track how fast Oreo was growing. Quintero remembers the vets later telling him it was “like he never had the disease.”

Quintero credits the daily log with helping him recognize how sick Oreo was and get him to the vet when he did.
