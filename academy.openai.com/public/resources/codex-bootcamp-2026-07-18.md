## Series overview

Codex Bootcamp is a three-part live series designed to help builders move from practical day-to-day Codex usage to repeatable team workflows and advanced automation. Each session builds on the last, but you can register for one or all three based on your experience and goals.

## What to expect

* Practical Codex workflows grounded in real codebases

* A progressive path from individual tasks to team and platform-level patterns

* Live demos, implementation guidance, and Q&A with OpenAI builders

* Reusable habits for safe, reviewable, production-ready development

## Who should attend

Technical builders, software engineers, and developer productivity, platform, infrastructure, security, and engineering teams using or exploring Codex.
