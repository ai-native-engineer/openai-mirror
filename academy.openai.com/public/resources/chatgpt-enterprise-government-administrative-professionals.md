Government administrative professionals keep offices moving—from leadership briefings and meeting coordination to correspondence, records, reporting, and event delivery.

This prompt pack offers practical, ready-to-use examples for turning authorized source material into clearer drafts, organized workflows, and review-ready work products with ChatGPT Enterprise. Each example reinforces source traceability, human review, and responsible handling of government information.

Created by Amanda Bullock.

Download the prompt pack and try one of the workflows with a task already on your plate.
