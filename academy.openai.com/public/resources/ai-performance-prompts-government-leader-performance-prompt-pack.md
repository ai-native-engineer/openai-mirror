## Government Leader Prompt Pack

Use ChatGPT Work as a reflection partner to help with conversations, coaching, STAR examples, SMART goals, ladders, improvement, and promotion.

How to use

DIRECTION

Replace bracketed text with your context. Paste only approved information into ChatGPT Work. Ask for facts, inferences, gaps, options, and language you can adapt in your own voice.

# Difficult conversations

USE WHEN

Address a performance gap, behavior issue, missed expectation, or trust breakdown.

PROMPT

Act as my preparation partner. Context: [role], [expectation], [facts], [impact], [prior feedback],

[desired behavior]. Draft a respectful opening, three questions to understand their view, two ways to

name the impact, and a closing commitment. Separate facts from interpretation.

# Coaching prompts

USE WHEN

Prepare a better 1:1 or development conversation.

PROMPT

Review this evidence and create a coaching plan. Identify strengths, one growth edge, the pattern

behind the issue, five coaching questions, one low-risk practice assignment, and the follow-up I

should schedule.

# STAR examples

USE WHEN

Create clean examples for reviews, calibration, awards, or promotion packets.

PROMPT

Convert these notes into three STAR examples. Label Situation, Task, Action, and Result. Quantify

impact where possible, name the behavior demonstrated, and flag missing evidence to confirm.

## SMART goals

USE WHEN

Turn a vague priority into measurable expectations.

PROMPT

Turn this priority into two SMART goals. Make each goal specific, measurable, achievable, relevant,

and timebound. Include leading indicators, lagging indicators, risks, and a weekly check-in question.

## Career ladder prompts

USE WHEN

Connect current evidence to level expectations.

PROMPT

Compare this employee's evidence to the career ladder for [level/role]. Show where evidence is

strong, where it is thin, what signals would demonstrate readiness, and what assignments would

build those signals.

## Performance improvement prompts

USE WHEN

Create a fair, documented, supportive improvement plan.

PROMPT

Draft a performance improvement support plan using [expectations], [evidence], [impact], [prior

support], [required change], and [timeline]. Include measurable milestones, manager support,

employee actions, and check-in cadence.

## Promotion prompts

USE WHEN

Assess readiness or prepare a balanced promotion case.

PROMPT

Assess promotion readiness against [criteria]. Organize evidence by scope, impact, consistency,

leadership, and next-level behaviors. Draft a balanced narrative, likely calibration questions, and

proof gaps.
