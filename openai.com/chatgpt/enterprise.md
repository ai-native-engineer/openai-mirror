### Marketing

Turn campaign briefs, docs, and performance data into leadership-ready readouts with clear recommendations for the next launch. ### Operations

Enter every weekly review with current risks, owners, and next steps—without chasing updates or rebuilding decks. ChatGPT Work can pull data from any project management system, initiative trackers, plan docs, and recent messages to prepare the review and help teams focus on unblocking priorities.

Run monthly close BvA and re-forecasting without manually stitching together data across systems. ChatGPT Work queries your data warehouse, combines the results with CRM data and business context, and builds an interactive report that explains what changed, recommends where to focus, and turns the findings into an action plan. [Learn more⁠](https://openai.com/business/solutions/data/).
