## Building Big Air

Eleven years ago Derek and Larissa Guetter created ATV Big Air Tour – a traveling ATV stunt show. They started as a small regional outfit, but quickly grew into one of the most successful motorsports tours in the country.

They wanted to continue growing their business, but their biggest challenge was having too much to do, and not enough manpower to do it. They were juggling the logistics of the show and the administrative tasks of running a small business, all while raising three young daughters. Most show days they were working until 3am just to keep up.

## Sound engineering

Show me the best way to set up my speakers onstage for a 250 ft wide covered grandstand.

Split the grandstand into three coverage zones: left, center, and right.

![Diagram showing a three-zone speaker setup for a 250-foot-wide grandstand.](//images.ctfassets.net/kftzwdyauwt9/5F7BC3AdsEq7Y3Y3HJFqXO/b8d87ad0fd39928964b74fe9f33b55fd/Screenshot_2026-08-25_at_12.06.00_PM.png?w=828&q=90&fm=webp "Sound engineering speaker setup diagram")

* Place one PS18 sub and one PS15 top about 20 feet left of center.
* Place a second PS18 and PS15 stack at center.
* Place the third PS18 and PS15 stack about 20 feet right of center.
* Aim the center top straight ahead and angle the outside tops slightly outward to cover the left and right seating zones.
* Use the fourth PS15 as a stage monitor or extra side fill if one outer section needs more coverage.

This should give you even coverage across the roughly 250-foot-wide grandstand while minimizing gaps between zones.

## Marketing plan

This week I have to get ready for our next event in Hays, Kansas. Can you help me put together a marketing plan for the event?

Absolutely. Here’s a practical marketing plan you can run in the week leading up to the Hays, Kansas show.

**Hays, Kansas marketing plan**

**Goal:** Build local awareness and attendance among families and motorsports fans.

**One week before**

* Announce the event on Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok.
* Share a stunt video with the date, location, and showtimes.
* Tag the venue, sponsors, riders, and local Hays organizations.
* Ask the coordinator to promote the show through their website, email, and social channels.

**During the week**

* Post rider spotlights, behind-the-scenes footage, and countdown reminders.
* Run a small paid social campaign within 60 miles of Hays.
* Share practical information, including admission, parking, weather, and showtimes.

**Show day**

* Post the schedule and capture setup, stunts, crowd reactions, giveaways, and sponsor signage.
* Encourage guests to tag #ATVBigAirTour.

**After the event**

* Share a highlight reel and thank the organizers, sponsors, and fans.
* Record attendance, social engagement, giveaways, and audience feedback for the client recap.
