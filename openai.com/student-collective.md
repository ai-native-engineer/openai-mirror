Students have been among the first to find new ways AI can support learning, creativity, and problem-solving. As OpenAI builds more capable tools, we believe students will continue leading the way and bring others along with them.

The OpenAI Student Collective gives undergraduate Campus Leads the tools, training, funding, and support to help that energy grow on campus.

We’re looking for students excited to bring peers from every field together, help them gain confidence with OpenAI tools, and showcase what they create with a global community.

**Applications are open through August 10, 2026 at 11:59pm PT. Learn more and** [**apply below**⁠](#apply)**.**

### How the program works

Campus Leads work in pairs to create a place where students learn the latest AI tools, start projects, and share what they create along the way.

### How to apply

Applications are now open through August 10, 2026 at 11:59pm PT.

Students from every discipline are encouraged to apply. We’re looking for undergraduate students who are curious, community-minded, and excited to introduce others to AI.

#### Qualifications

* You love ChatGPT, Codex, and OpenAI tools.
* You’re 18 years or older.
* You’re enrolled in an undergraduate college or university program as of August 2026 and expect to graduate after December 2027.
* You’re committed to 4–6 hours per week of participation from August 2026–June 2027.
* You study in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, France, Germany, India, Japan, or South Korea and have work authorization in your country of study. *Studying elsewhere? You can register your interest by submitting an application, and we’ll let you know when the Collective becomes available in your country.*
* You’re not currently an ambassador or intern for any other AI programs or companies.

### Can I apply if I haven’t used Codex or built with AI before?

Yes. You don’t need experience with Codex—or a portfolio of AI projects—to apply. We care more that you’re curious, eager to learn, and know how to bring people together. We’ll provide training and support to help you build confidence with new tools before introducing them on campus. Knowing what it feels like to be a beginner can also help you create a more welcoming experience for others who are just getting started.

### What happens after I apply?

Applications close on August 10, 2026. After reviewing applications, we’ll invite selected applicants to interview in August so we can learn more about their motivation, initiative, and community mindset. Accepted Campus Leads will complete onboarding before launching campus programming in the fall.

### Who can I invite to participate in the community on my campus?

Campus Leads will bring together students from across majors, experience levels, and campus communities. Events aren’t only for technical students or those already experimenting with AI—they should also feel welcoming and accessible to students who are just getting started. The goal is to create a space where anyone curious about AI can learn, experiment, and build alongside their peers.

### What is the overall time commitment for a Campus Lead?

The program runs from onboarding in late August 2026 through June 2027, with campus programming beginning in September. Campus Leads should expect to spend an average of 4–6 hours per week hosting events, participating in training and check-ins, and supporting their campus community. Some weeks will be lighter, while workshops and showcases may require more time.

### Will OpenAI provide workshop materials or do I need to create everything myself?

You won’t need to create everything from scratch. OpenAI will provide workshop guides, templates, training, and resources to help you plan and run sessions on your campus.

Every student community is different, so you’ll have room to adapt these materials and shape workshops around what your peers are most excited to learn or build.

### How long will the workshops be?

Most workshops should last one to two hours, though the format may vary depending on the topic and audience. We’ll provide flexible workshop guides that you can adapt to what students on your campus want to learn or build.

### Do I need to start an official club to become a Campus Lead?

No. You can apply and begin hosting events without establishing an official campus club. We’ll provide resources, programming ideas, funding, and support to help you bring students together from the start. If official recognition would make it easier to book rooms, reach students, or build credibility on your campus, we can also support you through that process.

### Can I request a specific co-lead?

We know that campus programming is most successful when two people can lean on each other and build together. We review every application closely and take care to pair each lead with a complementary co-lead. If you have a friend or classmate who you think complements your skill set and helps reach a different part of campus, we encourage you to each apply on your own and mention one another as part of your application.

### Can I participate in another ambassador program while being a Campus Lead?

Campus Leads cannot participate in another ambassador program while serving in this role. You’re still welcome to participate in campus clubs, student organizations, research groups, internships, and other leadership roles, as long as they don’t create a conflict or prevent you from meeting the program commitment.

### Can postgraduate students apply?

The Campus Lead role is currently open to undergraduate students who expect to graduate after December 2027. Postgraduate students are still welcome to attend campus events and workshops.

### How will I receive my stipend and when?

Campus Leads will be paid through our secure payment platform. We’ll share details during onboarding, including payment timing, payment method, and any required documentation.

Stipends will be paid at the end of each semester, after Campus Leads have participated in the program and completed the expected semester activities.
