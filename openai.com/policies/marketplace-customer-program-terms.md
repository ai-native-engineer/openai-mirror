# OpenAI Marketplace Customer Program Terms

The OpenAI Marketplace Program (the “Marketplace Program”) lets OpenAI customers (each, a “Customer”) purchase approved third-party products or services (each, an “Eligible Partner Product”) on the OpenAI Marketplace (the “Marketplace”) from participating third-party providers (“Partners”). Purchases made through the Marketplace Program may count toward Customer’s Commitment Amount identified in the applicable OpenAI Order Form, subject to these terms. Customer purchases each Eligible Partner Product under a separate agreement with the applicable Partner. These terms supplement the agreement governing Customer’s applicable OpenAI Order Form, together with that OpenAI Order Form (the “OpenAI Agreement”).

1. Eligibility and Minimum Commitment Amounts. To participate in the Marketplace Program, Customer must have an order form directly with OpenAI (an “OpenAI Order Form”) specifying a commitment amount of at least US$1,000,000, unless OpenAI agrees otherwise in writing, including by email. “Commitment Amount” means the amount specified under “Commitment Amount” in the applicable OpenAI Order Form. Eligible Partner Spend may be applied only toward commitments for OpenAI API and ChatGPT/Codex services expressly identified as eligible in the applicable OpenAI Order Form or an amendment to it.
2. Eligible Partner Spend. “Eligible Partner Spend” means amounts Customer actually pays for an Eligible Partner Product, net of discounts, rebates, credits, refunds and reversals, and subject to the timing rules below. Taxes, bank and financing charges, penalties, and charges for data, professional services, support or other unapproved products or services do not qualify. Purchases made before Customer’s participation date do not qualify unless approved by OpenAI, subject to any conditions specified in that approval. Purchases under an existing agreement with a Partner which precede Customer Marketplace eligibility do not qualify unless approved by OpenAI, subject to any conditions specified in that approval.
3. Buying a Partner Product. OpenAI will provide Customer with written confirmation of its eligibility to participate in the Marketplace Program, specifying Customer’s participation date and any applicable purchase conditions. Customer will provide that confirmation to the applicable Partner when arranging a purchase under the Marketplace Program. Each purchase remains subject to these terms and any applicable conditions specified in the confirmation. Partner and Customer will contract directly for the Eligible Partner Product, and Partner will invoice and collect payment directly from the Customer. Customer is responsible for applicable taxes on Eligible Partner Products, which Partner or OpenAI, as applicable, will charge when required by law. Customer may request application of Eligible Partner Spend toward its Commitment Amount through OpenAI’s designated confirmation process. OpenAI will not apply amounts for a transaction unless Customer has expressly authorized that application.
4. Commitment Period and Multiyear Purchases. “Commitment Period” means each annual period specified in the applicable OpenAI Order Form for Customer to satisfy its Commitment Amount. A multiyear OpenAI Order Form has a separate Commitment Period for each annual commitment. OpenAI will apply charges for no more than twelve months of a Partner subscription at a time. For a multiyear Partner subscription, each subscription year begins on the subscription’s start date or an anniversary of that date. Charges for each subsequent subscription year may be submitted no earlier than the start of that year, even if Customer prepaid those charges. Partner must submit or resubmit the applicable invoice identifying the charges for that subscription year. Each submission remains subject to the applicable Commitment Period, unused Marketplace Allowance and remaining Commitment Amount. The same charges cannot count more than once. Qualifying charges covering up to twelve months of a Partner subscription may be applied even if that subscription period extends beyond the applicable Commitment Period, provided OpenAI receives the invoice during that Commitment Period.
5. Marketplace Allowance. Unless otherwise agreed by OpenAI, Customer’s “Marketplace Allowance” equals 25% of its Commitment Amount for the applicable Commitment Period. This allowance applies collectively across all Partners, and amounts already applied count toward it. Subsequent invoices do not create a new allowance. For a multiyear OpenAI Order Form, a separate Marketplace Allowance applies to each Commitment Period. Any unused allowance expires at the end of that Commitment Period and cannot be transferred to another Commitment Amount or account.
6. How OpenAI Applies Spend. OpenAI will apply qualifying amounts shown on Partner invoices toward Customer’s Commitment Amount on a dollar-for-dollar basis, in the order OpenAI receives the invoices, subject to verification. Each application is limited to the unused Marketplace Allowance and the remaining Commitment Amount, after accounting for OpenAI service charges already incurred and Partner spend already applied. Application before Customer pays Partner remains subject to adjustment under these terms. Amounts applied reduce the portion of Customer’s Commitment Amount that remains to be satisfied. They do not pay Partner invoices, create OpenAI usage credits, reduce OpenAI service charges or entitle Customer to cash or a refund. Existing OpenAI prepaid balances and credits remain unchanged unless the OpenAI Order Form provides otherwise. Customer remains responsible for OpenAI service charges and any remaining commitment shortfall. OpenAI must receive the invoice during the applicable Commitment Period and on or after Customer’s participation date, unless OpenAI approves otherwise in writing.
7. Multiple Spend Commitments. If Customer has eligible Commitment Amounts under more than one OpenAI Order Form, OpenAI will determine which commitment an invoiced amount applies to, subject to the applicable Marketplace Allowance and Commitment Period. Customer may request a different allocation, subject to OpenAI’s approval. The same amount cannot count more than once.
8. Reporting and Verification. Partner will report qualifying transactions and payments to OpenAI. Customer authorizes OpenAI and Partner to exchange information reasonably necessary to verify and apply Eligible Partner Spend, including account identifiers, invoices, payment information and service dates. Customer will provide reasonable supporting information on request. Notwithstanding the confidentiality provisions of the OpenAI Agreement, Customer authorizes OpenAI to disclose the existence of that agreement and Customer’s eligibility for the Marketplace Program to a Partner from which Customer proposes to purchase. Customer is responsible for coordinating with Partner to ensure timely submission of invoices and supporting information required for application of Eligible Partner Spend.
9. Disputes and Adjustments. Customer will resolve disputes about Partner charges directly with Partner. OpenAI may withhold or adjust application of disputed amounts; undisputed amounts may qualify separately. OpenAI may also correct amounts applied for nonpayment when due, refunds, credits, cancellations, duplicate reporting or other errors and will notify Customer of the adjustment. Customer remains responsible under the OpenAI Agreement for any resulting commitment shortfall, including after the Commitment Period ends. If Customer subsequently pays an amount reversed for nonpayment, OpenAI may reapply the qualifying amount to the original Commitment Period, subject to verification and the applicable Marketplace Allowance.
10. Changes to Eligibility. OpenAI will notify Customer if an Eligible Partner Product no longer qualifies for new purchases or renewals under the Marketplace Program. Purchases approved before that notice will remain eligible until the end of the existing non-cancellable subscription term or the end of the applicable Commitment Period, whichever occurs first, subject to the Marketplace Allowance. OpenAI will not reverse amounts validly applied before the notice solely because the Partner or Eligible Partner Product later becomes ineligible.
11. Changes to Billing. If OpenAI begins collecting payments for Eligible Partner Products through the Marketplace, Customer’s existing purchases will continue to be billed directly by the applicable Partner unless Customer, Partner and OpenAI agree otherwise. Any transition will be subject to Customer’s acceptance of the applicable billing terms and an agreed effective date. Customer will not be billed twice for the same purchase, and the same Eligible Partner Spend will not be counted more than once toward a Commitment Amount. Payments made to OpenAI as Partner’s authorized collection agent under the applicable billing terms will be treated as payments to Partner for purposes of these terms.
12. Partner Products. Listing an Eligible Partner Product on the Marketplace or approving a purchase for participation in the Marketplace Program does not constitute OpenAI’s endorsement, warranty or recommendation of the Partner or its product. Customer’s purchase and use of an Eligible Partner Product are governed solely by Customer’s agreement with the applicable Partner. OpenAI is not a party to that agreement, and Partner is solely responsible for providing, supporting and maintaining its product. To the maximum extent permitted by law, OpenAI makes no warranties regarding Eligible Partner Products and disclaims liability arising from Customer’s purchase or use of those products or Partner’s acts or omissions. This provision does not limit OpenAI’s express obligations under these terms or the OpenAI Agreement.
13. Relationship to the OpenAI Agreement. These terms govern only Customer’s participation in the Marketplace Program. The OpenAI Agreement continues to govern Customer’s use of and access to the OpenAI services. If these terms conflict with a specific provision of the OpenAI Order Form, that OpenAI Order Form provision controls. Changes to an approved purchase’s allowance or spend calculation require Customer’s agreement.
