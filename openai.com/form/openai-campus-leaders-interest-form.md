# OpenAI campus leaders interest form

Join a community of student builders, scientists, and early adopters shaping how AI is used on their campuses.

This community is for students who know OpenAI's technology well and are excited to help peers learn, explore new tools, and stay close to the frontier of AI.

### Eligibility

* Currently enrolled at a university or college, with at least one full academic year remaining
* Aged 18 or above
* Able to communicate in English

### What campus leaders do

Campus leaders help bring practical AI learning to life on their campuses by supporting peer-led activities and community moments. Participants should be prepared to commit approximately **6–8 hours per month on average**.
