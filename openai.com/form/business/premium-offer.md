# Get up to $500 worth of credits toward your first ChatGPT Business Premium Seats

Premium seats are coming soon to [ChatGPT Business⁠(opens in a new window)](https://chatgpt.com/business/)[1](#citation-bottom-1). Up to 10,000 eligible workspaces that sign up before August 20, 2026 can receive $100 in workspace credits (2,500 credits) for each qualifying Premium seat they add, up to five seats[2](#citation-bottom-2).

Complete this short form and we’ll let you know when Premium becomes available to your workspace. New and existing ChatGPT Business workspace owners are eligible. If you plan to create a new Business workspace, sign up with the email address you’ll use as the workspace owner. Some eligible businesses may receive early access before general availability.

## Footnotes

1. 1

   Premium seats will cost $125 per user per month when billed monthly, or $100 per user per month billed annually. Standard seats remain $25 per user per month, or $20 per user per month billed annually.
2. 2

   Eligible ChatGPT Business workspace owners who join the Premium-seat waitlist before August 20, 2026 using their workspace owner email who add qualifying Premium Business seats within 30 days after launch will receive $100 in workspace service credits per qualifying seat, up to $500 per workspace. Credits expire one year after grant, cannot be applied to transaction-based taxes, and are nontransferable, nonrefundable except where required by law, and not redeemable for cash. This offer cannot be combined with other promotions, discounts, trials, or promotional credits. [OpenAI Service Credit Terms⁠](https://openai.com/policies/service-credit-terms/) apply. Learn more about how the promotion works in the [help center article⁠(opens in a new window)](https://help.openai.com/articles/20001420).
