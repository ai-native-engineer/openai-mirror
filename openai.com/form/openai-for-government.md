A new offer for US government organizations

# Get Started with OpenAI for Government

Public servants will have secure access to ChatGPT Enterprise, Codex, and OpenAI models—with $0 platform fee *(No seat or licenses costs)* and 50% off eligible usage through GSA OneGov.

Key program highlights:

* **$0 platform fee** discounted from $15/month
* **50% discount** on eligible first-party usage
* **27 month** program term
* **Agency-funded** – each agency places and funds its own order
* **Training and enablement** designed specifically for public servants
