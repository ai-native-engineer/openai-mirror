# OpenAI Economic Research Collaborators

The OpenAI Economic Research team works with economists and graduate students around the world to understand how AI is reshaping the economy. Through research collaborations and support for independent inquiry, we bring together diverse expertise to build evidence that helps people navigate AI-driven economic change.

## Economists in Residence

Our Economist in Residence program brings economists onto the team part-time to lead their own work and contribute their expertise to our research.

## The OpenAI Economic Research Exchange

A new research program advancing bold, independent inquiry into how AI is reshaping work, firms, institutions, and the global economy. Selected researchers pursue original  privacy-preserving projects with support from OpenAI’s Economic Research team to help build the evidence needed to understand and navigate AI-driven economic change. See the inaugural cohort below. To learn more about the Exchange, read the [blog](/index/introducing-the-openai-economic-research-exchange/).
