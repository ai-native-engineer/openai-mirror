WhisperTranscribe is an AI transcription app that converts audio, video, meetings, lectures, interviews, podcasts, voice memos, webinars, and YouTube links into accurate transcripts, subtitles, captions, and summaries inside ChatGPT. It supports speech to text, audio to text, video to text, meeting transcription, podcast transcription, lecture transcription, interview transcription, subtitle generation, progress tracking, recent job history, completed transcript review, and share links. It helps students, creators, journalists, researchers, podcasters, educators, and business teams turn spoken content into searchable text, meeting notes, subtitles, and reusable content.
