Transcribe any recording, or search millions of moments from podcasts, city-council and county meetings, and Supreme Court hearings — with new episodes and meetings added every day.
Attach an audio file in the chat, or paste a direct link or a Google Drive, Dropbox or OneDrive share link, and get a private, speaker-attributed transcript you can question. Every recording you add goes into your own workspace, where the people, companies, and topics in it are linked into a knowledge graph — so the tenth recording is searchable alongside the first.
Ask what was said about anything and get answers with the speaker, the exact timestamp, and a link that jumps to the moment. Track what any person, company, or topic has said across every episode and every recording of your own.
