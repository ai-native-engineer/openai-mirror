Circleback is an AI notetaker that captures and organizes everything from your conversations. It records and transcribes your meetings, writes clear notes, and assigns action items.
It also lets you build automations that pull custom info from each meeting and update other apps—your CRM, issue tracker, Slack.
You can ask it questions across all your team’s conversations, connect your AI agents, and use it as your company brain that remembers everything.
Use the Circleback ChatGPT app to search and retrieve context from meeting notes, transcripts, calendar events, action items, people, companies, support articles, and connected email threads.
