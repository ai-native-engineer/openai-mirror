Transkriptor brings your transcription library into ChatGPT.
Work with the transcripts and recordings already in your Transkriptor account—
list them, read the full text, get AI-generated summaries, download as PDF,
and check your account details, all from a chat conversation.
Key features:
- Browse transcripts: List and filter your transcriptions by file name keyword,
date range, or duration.
- Read full transcripts: Fetch the complete transcript content, including
speaker labels and timestamps.
- AI summaries: Get concise, structured summaries of meetings, interviews,
lectures, podcasts, and other recorded content.
- PDF export: Generate a downloadable PDF of any transcript.
- Account overview: Check remaining transcription minutes, subscription plan,
and language preference.
To create new transcriptions, use Transkriptor on the web (transkriptor.com),
iOS, or Android. Once a transcription exists in your account, this connector
makes it easy to find, read, summarize, and share through ChatGPT.
