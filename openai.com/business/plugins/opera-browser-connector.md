Connect Opera Browser to ChatGPT. When enabled, it can see open tabs, read page content, take screenshots of the page, and navigate to the page. And, based on the permissions you allow it, it could also view browsing history and close tabs
First ensure that Opera Browser Connector is enabled in Opera;
Opera browser settings > search for "AI Services" > On Browser Connector click install
Once installed, the connector appears in Opera's toolbar, where you can manage connections to ChatGPT
