Turn anything into a narrated explainer video, without leaving ChatGPT. Free to start, no login required.
Give it a topic, a document, a webpage, a codebase or a product brief, and Explain turns it into a video in your language — with voiceover, diagrams, animations and images, plus real code walkthroughs when you need them.
Add your own images and screenshots and they go straight in. Share with a link or download the video file.
How you can use Explain:
\* Onboard new employees without filming a thing
\* Study a difficult subject before an exam, in the language you think in
\* Walk your team through a pull request or codebase
\* Show customers how your product works, using your own screenshots
\* Make the same video in 40+ languages from a single document
\* Start your class with a 3-minute lesson opener built from the textbook chapter
\* Paste any web link to get a narrated video explanation
\* Turn a long ChatGPT conversation into a video you can rewatch or share
\* Create a bedtime story for your child
Want to make it your own? Keep editing on our platform, then combine your explainers into full courses and playlists.
