# JobTread

Manage construction projects

Connect ChatGPT directly to your JobTread account to manage your construction business through natural conversation. This MCP server gives ChatGPT secure access to your JobTread data, so you can get real-time answers, make updates, and stay on top of your projects without switching between apps.
With the JobTread MCP server in ChatGPT, you can:
• Jobs & Projects: Look up job details, check statuses, create new jobs, and update project information.
• Estimates & Budgets: Review estimates, pull line-item breakdowns, and track budget vs. actual costs.
• Schedules: View upcoming tasks and milestones, check timelines, and keep projects moving forward.
• Customers & Contacts: Search client records, review communication history, and manage contact info.
• Vendors & Subcontractors: Access vendor profiles and coordinate with trade partners.
• Documents & Daily Logs: Reference job records, daily logs, and reports to keep your team aligned.
Whether you’re a builder, remodeler, or specialty contractor, the JobTread MCP server turns ChatGPT into a hands-on operations assistant, answering prompts like “What’s the status of the Henderson kitchen remodel?” or “Show me all open estimates this month” in seconds.
Your data stays protected through JobTread’s authenticated API, and ChatGPT only accesses what you explicitly allow.
