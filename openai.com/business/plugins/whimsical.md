Whimsical app extends ChatGPT with a powerful whiteboard:
· Ask @whimsical to generate beautiful flowcharts, mind maps, wireframes, sequence diagrams, sticky notes or other type of visual content based on your current conversation.
· Update it with follow-up prompts or open in Whimsical app to edit, share and collaborate.
· Search and reference existing whiteboards from your Whimsical workspace.
