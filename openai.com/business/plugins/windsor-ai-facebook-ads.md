Windsor.ai Facebook Ads helps marketers analyze live Facebook Ads and Meta Ads performance in ChatGPT. Users can discover connected ad accounts, inspect valid fields and attribution options, query campaign, ad set, and ad metrics, connect the Facebook connector, and safely run supported Meta Ads actions such as creating, pausing, enabling, or changing budgets after confirmation.
