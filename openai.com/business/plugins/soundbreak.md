SoundBreak is the world's first fully licensed AI music platform where you can write songs with the official AI versions of your favorite artists and songwriters. Built with artists, not on them.
+ Co-write original songs with licensed AI artists and songwriters
+ Submit your songs directly to artists and the SoundBreak Radio program
+ Distribute your music to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and more—all from SoundBreak
+ Ethically trained AI models where artists share in subscription and distribution revenue
