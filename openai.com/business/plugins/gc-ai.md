GC AI brings your legal work into ChatGPT. Connect your GC AI workspace and put the AI built for in-house legal teams right in the chat.
Ask a legal question and get an answer grounded in your own documents. Draft a contract or a memo. Review and redline an agreement against your standards. Run the playbooks your team already relies on. Whatever you start here stays connected to your GC AI workspace.
With GC AI you can:
Ask legal questions and get answers grounded in your documents
Draft contracts, memos, and emails
Review and redline agreements
Run your team’s playbooks and automations
Work with the files and projects in your workspace
