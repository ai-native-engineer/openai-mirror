# Descrybe Legal Engine

Primary law research toolkit

Descrybe Legal Engine brings U.S. primary-law research tools into ChatGPT. Use it to search legal authority, resolve citations, retrieve case details and source passages, check case treatment and citing authorities, and verify quoted legal language. It helps legal professionals and serious legal researchers move from a question to primary legal sources they can read and verify.
