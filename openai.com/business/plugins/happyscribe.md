Connect HappyScribe to give ChatGPT on-demand access to your organization's conversational memory: every transcript, meeting recording, interview, and quote, speaker-attributed, verbatim, in 150+ languages.
HappyScribe is an AI notetaker and transcription platform that captures every conversation: online meetings, in-person recordings, interviews, fieldwork, uploaded media. It turns them into searchable, citable, structured knowledge with verbatim accuracy: what was actually said, by whom, in what language.
What ChatGPT can do with HappyScribe connected:
- Search your library by keyword, speaker, date, or topic
- Pull exact quotes with verified timestamps and deep links to the editor
- Transcribe new media from a URL (YouTube, Vimeo, direct links)
- Manage folders, rename speakers, and edit transcript text
- Diagnose why a meeting wasn't recorded
- Explore the people and companies graph across your recordings
Best-in-class multilingual accuracy across 150+ languages. European and GDPR-native.
