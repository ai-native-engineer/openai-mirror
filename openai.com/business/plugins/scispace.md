SciSpace for ChatGPT supports the core tasks of research: discovery, triage, and synthesis.
Use it to search for academic papers and review them in a customizable table with metadata and links. Add structured columns - abstract, summary, methods, results, conclusions - to quickly assess relevance and quality. Then use the collected list as the basis for literature review notes, thematic synthesis, and targeted follow-up searches.
Get started with prompts like:
1. "SciSpace Find papers on impact of nanoplastics on gut health"
2. "SciSpace How does climate change impact biodiversity?"
