Describe what you need and WordPress builds it: a portfolio, a restaurant menu, a blog, an online store. Tell WordPress about your business, your audience, what pages you need. It plans the site, writes the content, picks a design, and launches it on WordPress.com.
Already have a WordPress.com site? Connect it to publish and schedule posts, check your traffic, moderate comments, manage plugins, and update settings. 70+ abilities across your full site.
WordPress.com's plugin connects ChatGPT or Codex directly to WordPress.com, so you can build a new site or manage an existing one — all through conversation.
If you are running locally in Codex, you can also connect to WordPress Studio for local site builds, theme and plugin development, site edits and site administration.
