Windsor.ai Google Ads helps marketers analyze live Google Ads performance. Users can discover connected ad accounts, inspect valid fields and segmentation options, query campaign, ad group, and keyword metrics, connect the Google Ads connector, and safely run supported Google Ads actions such as creating, pausing, enabling, and updating campaigns, ad groups, ads, and keywords, setting budgets, managing bidding, and managing Customer Match audience lists, after confirmation. Windsor.ai is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the platforms it connects to.
