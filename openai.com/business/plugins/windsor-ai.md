Windsor.ai connects your marketing and business data sources to your AI assistant, so you can ask questions and take action in plain English.
It pulls performance data from any connected source, and on supported ad and marketing platforms it can make changes directly, such as pausing or enabling a campaign, setting a budget, creating a Google Business Profile post, or replying to a review, always after you confirm.
Common workflows:
- Compare spend, clicks, and conversions across every connected ad account
- Find and pause an underperforming Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, LinkedIn Ads, or Microsoft Ads campaign
- Post or reply as your Instagram, Threads, Facebook, or Google Business Profile account
- Pull GA4, Google Search Console, or YouTube Analytics data straight into the conversation
- Sync HubSpot, Salesforce, or Shopify records
- Update a Klaviyo flow or an Amazon Seller Central listing
Connects to 350+ sources across advertising, analytics, organic social, CRM, e-commerce, email, and payments, including Google Ads, Meta (Facebook) Ads, Instagram, TikTok Ads, LinkedIn Ads, LinkedIn Company Pages, Microsoft Ads (Bing), GA4, Google Search Console, YouTube, Google Business Profile, HubSpot, Salesforce, Shopify, Klaviyo, Amazon Ads, Google Merchant Center, Stripe, and GoHighLevel.
Reading data only requires connecting the relevant account once. Making a change (pausing a campaign, posting content, replying to a review) always asks for your confirmation first, and it is only available on platforms Windsor explicitly supports write access for. Every other connected source is read-only. Free plan available, no credit card required.
Windsor.ai is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the platforms it connects to.
