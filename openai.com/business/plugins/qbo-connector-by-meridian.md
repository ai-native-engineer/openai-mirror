This connector lets ChatGPT and Codex securely read from and write to QuickBooks Online.
Some things you can do:
• Connect multiple QuickBooks Online companies to the same agent (great for booking intercompany JEs)
• Choose read-only or read-write access separately for each company
• Create arbitrary QuickBooks transactions, including journal entries
• Bulk-create invoices or import credit card transactions
• Upload and attach receipts and supporting files to QuickBooks transactions
• Automatically fill in your firm-specific reporting template
• The craziest use case I tried was "Clone the contents of QBO A into QBO B", and… it worked
