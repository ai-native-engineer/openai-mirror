WPWriter connects ChatGPT to WordPress so users can manage their sites directly from a chat window.
Instead of jumping between WordPress admin screens, SEO plugins, media tools, and separate AI apps, users can ask ChatGPT to create, edit, schedule, publish, and optimize content in natural language.
With WPWriter, users can manage posts, pages, categories, tags, menus, SEO metadata, featured images, media, plugins, themes, and publishing status. They can also set up autoblogging workflows with topic queues and publishing schedules, so new posts can be generated and published automatically.
WPWriter is built for bloggers, creators, agencies, marketers, and WordPress site owners who want a faster way to run their WordPress sites while staying in control of every change.
