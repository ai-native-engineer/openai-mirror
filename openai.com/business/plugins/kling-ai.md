Create posters, illustrations, portraits, product photos, e-commerce hero images, product listing images, product detail images, ad creatives, product showcase videos, marketing clips, e-commerce short videos, social media videos, and cinematic content for Taobao, Tmall, JD.com, Pinduoduo, Douyin, Kuaishou, Xiaohongshu, TikTok, Amazon, Shopify, and more. Paste or attach reference images in the conversation; Codex handles the CLI runtime, browser OAuth, local image paths, credit checks, generation submission, progress tracking, task status, and result retrieval. Requires a local Codex environment with Node.js 18+, Kling CLI 0.2.0+, and shell access. Supports explicit asset uploads, account switching, tool discovery, and transparent, user-authorized issue feedback.
