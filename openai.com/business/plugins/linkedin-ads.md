LinkedIn Ads helps advertisers turn every marketing dollar into business growth by making ad set performance easier to understand and act on. Understand what’s working. Build confidence. Make smarter marketing decisions—right where you already plan and analyze. Connect your LinkedIn Ads account with ChatGPT to get started.
This app is currently in beta and supports a limited set of LinkedIn Ads functionality. It provides read‑only access to ad set performance to help you understand results. Supported features and use cases may evolve as we expand tool coverage.
In this beta, you can:
- View and search ad accounts and ad sets you have access to
- Ask natural-language questions about ad set and ad performance
- Break down metrics by date range, ad set, or ad
Ask questions like:
- "Show me all my active ad sets from account X"
- “How did my ad sets from account X perform last week?”
- “Which ads have the highest CTR in ad set Y?”
