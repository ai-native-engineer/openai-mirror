Connect Brevo to analyze email campaign performance and A/B test results, look up contacts, and explore audience segments in plain language, all without leaving your conversation. ChatGPT can also generate HTML email templates, draft email campaigns, and draft SMS campaigns, saved directly to your Brevo account for you to review before sending. ChatGPT will never send or schedule email campaigns, and will never delete anything on your behalf. Available on all Brevo plans.
