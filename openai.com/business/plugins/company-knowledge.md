Company Knowledge helps you find decisions, documents, owners, and recent project updates across your company's connected sources. Ask a question and get a concise answer with citations so you can review where the information came from.
Use it to catch up on a project, find the right document, understand why a decision was made, or identify who owns the next step. Depending on your workspace setup, sources can include Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Drive, SharePoint, Notion, GitHub, Linear, email, calendars, and meeting notes.
