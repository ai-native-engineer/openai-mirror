## How it works

Build and manage WordPress sites through a conversation. Describe your business, portfolio, or store, and get a complete site tailored to your needs. You can also work on a site you already have to refine pages, update menus, connect a domain, check your traffic, and update your design.
Here are some ways to start:
- “Show me my top 10 posts this month and what they have in common.”
- “Look through the images in my site’s media library, find which are missing alt text, and give me suggestions for each.”
- “Set up a scheduled task: Every Monday, summarize my site’s traffic and flag anything that changed.”
You stay in control. Choose what your agent can do on each site and whether it asks before every change or goes ahead on its own.
Works with any site built on WordPress.com or through the Jetpack plugin. Authenticate through WordPress.com to set up the connection and start chatting.
