Peer legal insights for in-house counsel from The L Suite, the leading in-house counsel community.   Get peer advice, find counsel, issue-spot contracts, benchmarks and more. Members Only.
Lloyd by The L Suite brings peer intelligence from a private community of 2,500+ in-house general counsel and their teams into ChatGPT. Ask what in-house peers have done or said on any legal topic — negotiation approaches, precedent, playbooks, and benchmarks — and get attributed insights with citations and community sources (get\_peer\_insights), or source the legal tooling, service providers, and outside counsel that peers actually use (find\_vendors). Results are scoped to the member's own L Suite peer networks — exactly what they see on-platform. Read-only; no records are created or modified.
