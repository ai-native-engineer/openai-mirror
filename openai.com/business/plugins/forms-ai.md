Forms are easy to need and annoying to build. Every question type, every section break, every setting is another click - and by the time responses arrive, reading them is its own chore. This plugin moves all of it into the conversation.
Build a form by describing it. Say what you're collecting — a customer satisfaction survey, an event signup, a job application screener, a 20-point quiz for your class — and it's created in your account, with sensible question types chosen for you: multiple choice where the answers are known, scales for ratings, short text where they aren't, required fields where they matter.
Keep editing in plain language. Add a question in the middle. Reword the confusing one. Move it to page two. Make question 4 worth double. Drop the one nobody answers. Every change lands on the live form — no rebuilding from scratch, no digging through the editor to find the right toggle.
Set it up properly, without hunting for settings. Turn a form into a graded quiz, collect email addresses, limit it to one response per person, open or close it to responses - all by asking.
Then actually read the results. Find any of your forms by name, pull the responses, and ask real questions of them: What's the overall sentiment? What are the three most common complaints in the open-text box? How did answers differ between the two groups? Free-text feedback - the part that usually goes unread because summarizing 200 answers by hand isn't happening - gets read and summarized for you.
Works with the forms you already own, not just new ones.
