Quickly and easily integrate NetSuite to ChatGPT. The NetSuite AI Connector Service provides a protocol-driven integration service supporting Model Context Protocol (MCP), that gives customers a flexible and scalable way to connect AI assistants to NetSuite. Once connected, ChatGPT can directly access and interact with NetSuite data and functionality – quickly unlocking insights, generating reports, streamlining processes., and more. A comprehensive set of tools enable interactions and maintains NetSuite’s existing role-based security model.
