Demandbase integration with ChatGPT and Codex gives sales, marketing, and GTM teams seamless access to rich B2B data directly within ChatGPT and Codex for faster account research, prospecting, and engagement.
This app connects ChatGPT and Codex to Demandbase via a secure MCP connection, giving you access to both first-party and third-party data.
Third-Party Data (3P): Search and retrieve industry-leading company and contact data from Demandbase, including firmographics, technographics, family trees, company news, and leadership profiles.
First-Party Data (1P): Securely access your organization's Demandbase data, including known accounts, contacts, CRM entities, engagement, in-flight opportunities, intent signals, and buying-group data.
Account Brief: Generate a single, consolidated account overview combining engagement state, opportunities, buying-group coverage, intent signals, company profile, and recent news in a single request.
What you can do
Search companies by name, location, size, revenue, or industry
Discover decision-makers and influencers by role or job level
Fetch full company profiles, including recent news and competitors
Tap into 1P signals such as engagement, MQA status, and account activity
Identify buying intent on specific topics or keyword sets over time
Find net-new buying-group contacts that aren't already in your CRM
Pull a complete account brief in a single request for fast meeting preparation
Who it's for
Sales reps looking for fast answers about target accounts in real time
Marketing teams enriching leads and refining personas
RevOps and Enablement teams embedding GTM intelligence into workflows
AI product teams building agent-powered workflows
Availability
This integration is available to Demandbase customers with the appropriate Demandbase licenses and entitlements.
To request access, please contact your Demandbase Customer Success Manager (CSM).
For technical support, contact support@demandbase.com.
