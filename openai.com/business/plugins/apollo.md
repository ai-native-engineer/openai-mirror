Search and qualify accounts and contacts using Apollo’s data, enrich records, summarize key context, and take action (e.g., create lists, log notes, generate tasks, and draft or assemble outbound using sequences) using your team’s rules and permissions. Designed for speed and control: ChatGPT can pull only what you ask for, respect workspace access, and produce outputs that are ready to review, personalize, and ship.
