Access Google Meet, Zoom, Teams, and in-person meeting transcripts. Yours, and those from your team & company.
You can use the tl;dv connector to:
- Browse, search & summarize meeting transcripts from your own meetings: “Summarize all my To Dos from the past 7 days”
- Score all Sales Calls across your Sales Team against your Playbook: “Score all our Sales Calls from this month against the MEDDICC Framework.”
- Generate Follow-Up Emails: “Summarize my last Meeting with the Prospect at ACME Corp and generate a Follow-Up Email.”
- Analyze company-wide Customer calls: “Create a Voice of the Customer report across all customer-facing conversations and find upsell opportunities and identify churn risk.”
And way more
