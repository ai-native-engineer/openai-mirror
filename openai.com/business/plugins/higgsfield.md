Create images, videos, branded content, and websites with Higgsfield in ChatGPT. Start with an idea or reference image, choose a creative model or preset, and refine your results through conversation.
With Higgsfield, you can:
• Generate images and videos from text or reference images. Create portraits, illustrations, cinematic scenes, or bring a still photo to life.
• Browse image and video presets, or apply a named preset such as @Higgsfield /main-character or /brand-monument to a reference image.
• Create product photoshoots, brand identities, packaging mockups, social graphics, YouTube thumbnails, and Instagram covers.
• Use Ad Multiplier to create independent variations of an existing video ad. Change selected people, products, clothing, objects, backgrounds, or text while preserving untargeted elements.
• Make AI-generated UGC-style content, including product showcases, unboxings, tutorials, and fashion try-ons. Create website and app promos with a virtual presenter and real screenshots.
• Produce faceless videos, narrated explainers, and episodes with a consistent AI presenter. Write complete YouTube scripts, generate spoken narration, and add subtitles.
• Edit footage and create motion graphics with animated text, layouts, transitions, and effects. Receive rendered videos and editable project files for supported workflows.
• Build and deploy websites, web apps, and browser games. Create visual assets, refine the design, and update your hosted project through chat.
