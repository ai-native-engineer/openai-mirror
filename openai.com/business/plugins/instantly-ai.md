:   Instantly.ai

Instantly.ai helps users manage Instantly.ai email accounts, campaigns, leads, inbox activity, analytics, and background jobs through ChatGPT. The app supports listing and inspecting workspace data, creating and updating campaigns and lead lists, importing and moving leads, managing sender accounts, replying to email threads, verifying email deliverability, and checking campaign or warmup performance.
