Krisp AI Meeting Assistant MCP gives your AI assistant secure access to your meeting history, including transcripts, summaries, action items, and upcoming meetings. It enables your AI to work with real context from your actual conversations.
Put your meetings to work:
Draft documents and proposals using decisions, feedback, and stakeholder input from past meetings. Prepare for upcoming meetings with quick briefs on previous discussions, pending items, and attendees. Track follow ups by surfacing open action items across all meetings, filtered by assignee, status, or date. Search for specific moments by topic, attendee, keyword, or date range. Build presentations and reports by combining insights from multiple conversations in one place.
How it works:
Your AI assistant searches and retrieves information from your Krisp meetings in real time. Ask in plain language, such as “What did we decide about pricing last week?” or “Show my open action items,” and get answers grounded in what was actually discussed. Full transcripts are available whenever you need deeper context.
Why it’s different:
Krisp captures complete meeting transcripts with noise cancelled audio clarity, so your AI works from accurate and comprehensive records. Instead of static notes, your meetings become a structured, searchable knowledge base your AI assistant can use whenever you need it.
