## How it works

Simplify contract management with intelligent automation powered by Docusign. Go beyond simple execution to make complex agreement workflows effortless. Use natural language to create and send a contract, instantly surface insights like renewal dates and key obligations, and automate workflows that keep your business moving faster and with less friction.

### Prioritize agreements needing action

Find envelopes waiting on you and summarize the actions needed to move agreements forward.

### Find customer contract details

Search customer agreements and review signing status, recipients, and key dates in one place.

### Track renewals and obligations

Extract renewal dates and key obligations from completed agreements to support contract planning.

## Sample prompts

Find agreements needing action

Find Docusign envelopes waiting on me and summarize the action needed for each one.

Review customer agreements

Find agreements with Acme Corp and list each agreement's signing status, recipients, and key dates.

Review renewals and obligations

Review envelopes completed last month and list any renewal dates and key obligations, grouped by agreement.
