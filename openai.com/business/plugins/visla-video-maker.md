Visla Video Maker helps users create narrated videos from scripts drafted in ChatGPT. Users can share a video idea or source materials such as text, documents, PDFs, or URLs, and ChatGPT generates a script from that input. Visla then uses the script to create a narrated video with stock footage and background music. Users can open the generated project on Visla from the ChatGPT conversation to continue editing, export it, or download the final video. A Visla account is required to register and sign in to use these features.
