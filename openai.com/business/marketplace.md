### What is OpenAI Marketplace?

OpenAI Marketplace helps enterprise customers discover partner products and use part of their existing OpenAI commitment toward eligible products.

### Can I use my existing OpenAI commitment toward partner products?

Eligibility depends on your OpenAI agreement, the partner product and the program’s terms. Contact us to confirm whether you can use part of your existing commitment and how much you can apply.

### Which partner products are eligible?

Explore the products listed above, then contact us to confirm which are eligible for your organization. Eligibility applies to specific products; not every product from a listed partner qualifies.

### How do purchasing and billing work?

Contact us to confirm eligibility and how much of your existing OpenAI commitment you can use toward a partner product. You contract with and pay the partner directly; OpenAI reconciles use of your commitment under the program’s terms.

### How can my company participate as a partner?

Join our waitlist to tell us about your product. Our team will review eligibility and next steps. Participation is subject to eligibility and approval.
