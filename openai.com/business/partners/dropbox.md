### What is a Dropbox plugin?

Plugins help ChatGPT  connect with the tools, context, and workflows your team already uses. The Dropbox plugin in ChatGPT helps your team search across your Dropbox within ChatGPT. You can get answers grounded in your existing content, then turn insights into plans and deliverables you can share to keep work moving.

### Where can I find the plugin?

Go to the plugin section in ChatGPT and search Dropbox. Once you’ve found the plugin, click Install and authenticate with your account. Once you’ve authenticated, users can invoke a plugin by describing what they want, or explicitly by using the @ flow to select a plugin.

### What can I do once the Dropbox plugin is connected?

Find the right file, summarize key details, draft new content, save outputs back to Dropbox, and create sharing links for teammates or clients.

### What permissions do I need to use the plugin?

Your workspace admin must have the plugin turned on for you and your team. Once you find Your existing Dropbox and ChatGPT permissions stay in place, so ChatGPT only takes action with your confirmation.
