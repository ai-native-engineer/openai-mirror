### How is my data handled when I use a plugin?

Plugins connect ChatGPT to the tools and services you already use, helping you get more done. When a plugin needs information to complete your request, that information is shared with the provider and handled under its privacy and data-retention policies. Before connecting, you can review the plugin’s permissions and privacy policy to understand what it can access and how it handles your data.

### What permissions do I need to use the plugin?

Your ChatGPT workspace admin may need to enable the plugin before you can install or use it. You’ll also need an account with the partner and the appropriate permissions to access any relevant data or actions. Your existing permissions remain in place, and ChatGPT only takes supported actions with your confirmation.

### What is a plugin?

A plugin connects ChatGPT with a partner’s app or service so you can use that partner’s information and capabilities directly in ChatGPT.

### What are skills?

Skills are ready-to-use workflows or instructions designed for a partner’s plugin. They combine the partner’s expertise with ChatGPT to help you complete common tasks or where you want standardization in output.

### How do plugins work with ChatGPT?

In ChatGPT, you install a plugin and connect your account, then ChatGPT can use it when it is relevant to your request. Plugins are most powerful in ChatGPT Work, where they can take action on your behalf and complete tasks, all within the permissions you and your workspace admin have approved and granted.
