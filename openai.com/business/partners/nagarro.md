## About

Shaping the company of tomorrow

Nagarro is a global AI-native engineering and transformation company. We help enterprises design, build, and operate technology systems that keep their business in flow so they can adapt faster, operate more efficiently, and deliver measurable outcomes.

Our work is anchored in Engineering Intelligence, our AI-native framework for enterprise transformation. Every organization runs on flows across people, processes, and systems. When these flows break or slow down, performance suffers. We engineer intelligence for measurable AI Impact, combining AI-native engineering, grounded models, and human judgment. The result is sustained performance improvement, typically delivering at least a 20% uplift.

We operate at the intersection of engineering, technology, and the enterprise. We do not replace core systems. We unlock their full potential. From pilot to production, from ambition to impact, we move organizations from promise to performance in months, not years.

Our approach is guided by our CARING culture, a human-centric, people-first philosophy rooted in ethics, responsibility, and long-term partnership. It shapes how we work with clients and with each other, and it is why many of our client relationships span more than a decade.

With around 18,500 experts across 40 countries, Nagarro partners with leading enterprises, independent software vendors, and public-sector organizations across Europe, North America, and beyond. Together, we help organizations become AI-native, human-centric, and ready for continuous change.
