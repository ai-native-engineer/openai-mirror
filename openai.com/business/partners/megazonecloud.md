## About

MegazoneCloud is an AI and cloud native company and enterprise AI orchestrator, with 2,000 specialists driving AI transformation for over 8,000 customers in Korea and abroad. With major CSPs, 200 ISV partners and in-house solutions, it works end to end, from strategy, data and infrastructure to building and running AI models and agents. For OpenAI adopters, that means use case discovery and PoC, production rollout, licensing and billing, security and compliance, and ongoing operations. Spun off in 2018, it topped KRW 1 trillion (USD 700M) in 2022 revenue and holds 25 AWS, Microsoft and Google certifications. Across 10 countries, it helps enterprises turn multi-agent AI into results.
