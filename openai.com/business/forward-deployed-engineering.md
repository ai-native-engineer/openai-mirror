Disconnected systems and manual handoffs slow down customer service and internal operations. [OpenAI Presence](/business/openai-presence/) connects AI agents to your systems and policies to handle conversations and tasks, with testing, access controls, and human oversight.

#### Enterprise context

Business knowledge is scattered across systems, documents, and teams. Context Layer gives agents access to your data, definitions, and expertise through a shared foundation teams can improve and reuse across workflows.

Understanding how work happens across apps and systems takes time. Turn workflow recordings into process maps that show where teams can simplify steps, reduce rework, or automate tasks.

### What does an OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) do?

OpenAI Forward Deployed Engineers work with your team to understand important workflows, connect relevant information and systems, build and evaluate solutions, and help bring them into production. We pair hands-on OpenAI engineering with your team’s domain expertise.

### What is DeployCo?

DeployCo is an enterprise-focused, standalone subsidiary of OpenAI. It works to bridge the gap between AI experimentation and full-scale corporate production by embedding technical teams directly into enterprise businesses.

### How are DeployCo and OpenAI FDE connected?

OpenAI and OpenAI DeployCo work together to turn OpenAI technology into business value. OpenAI FDE connects customer engagements with OpenAI’s Product and Research teams, and DeployCo brings additional engineering and deployment capacity to support implementation at scale.

Together, we help customers access the expertise and support suited to their transformation goals, from developing new capabilities to expanding successful deployments across their business.

### Do FDEs provide professional services?

No. FDEs are customer-facing engineers who work alongside professional service partners to solve problems at the frontier of AI.

They work closely with your business, deployment partners, and OpenAI’s Product and Research teams.

If it can already be done with existing AI capabilities, an FDE typically isn’t needed.

### How is an FDE different from other go-to-market (GTM) roles?

FDEs complement the broader go-to-market (GTM) team with hands-on engineering where the path to production is still uncertain.

They build alongside your team, testing approaches and taking solutions into production. When the work requires new capabilities, they collaborate directly with OpenAI’s Product and Research teams. Their focus is on delivering measurable business outcomes and making successful solutions easier to repeat.

### What kinds of problems are a good fit?

Problems that are a good fit for OpenAI FDE include:

1. **An important part of your business is slow or costly, and the right AI approach isn’t clear:** For example, shortening the cycle from a product design to something you can manufacture.
2. **Your use case demands more than today’s models reliably deliver:** For example, a customer-service voice agent needs to select the right tools and follow complex instructions accurately enough for production.
3. **The same valuable problem keeps recurring across teams:** For example, engineering teams each build their own AI debugging tools, creating custom systems that are difficult to maintain and extend.

Strong engagements have a clear business objective, engaged domain experts, access to the relevant systems, and a practical way to measure progress.

### How does customer work inform OpenAI’s products and research?

Customer deployments show us what works in practice and where models or products still fall short. FDEs bring those learnings to our product and research teams, helping turn successful approaches into reusable capabilities and real-world challenges into areas for improvement.

### How do you measure success?

At the start, we define a measurable operational outcome with your team (such as turnaround time, quality, throughput, or manual effort). We then test the system on representative work and against the requirements for production.

### What happens after launch?

After the launch, OpenAI FDE sets up your team to own the newly-developed capability.

We also equip your team with the knowledge to improve and extend it. OpenAI and qualified partners can provide continued support and expansion.

### How can I become a FDE?

To become an OpenAI FDE you need to be passionate about solving hard problems and be technically skilled. FDEs come from varied backgrounds from generalists to domain experts and excel in their respective fields.
