## Use cases

#### Customer support

Build trusted AI support agents grounded in your SOPs, policies, and escalation rules. Resolve common requests across voice and chat, freeing teams for complex cases.

#### Demand generation

Engage inbound prospects instantly across voice and chat. Qualify leads, capture intent, and enrich records so sales teams can focus on the best opportunities.

#### Claims

Guide customers through the claims process, from initial intake to resolution. Collect required information, apply claims policies, update systems, and route complex or higher-risk cases to specialists.

#### Human resources

Give employees fast answers on leave, benefits, payroll, and expenses. Resolve cases and guide new hires through onboarding, from forms and equipment to training and account setup.

#### IT help desk

Resolve common IT requests, from password resets and account access to software and device support. Apply security policies, take approved actions, and route complex issues to the right teams.

#### Procurement

Streamline supplier onboarding and purchase-order requests from intake to approval. Apply procurement policies, collect required information, and route exceptions to the right teams.
