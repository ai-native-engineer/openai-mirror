# Shopping from Shopify merchants in ChatGPT

Find Shopify’s guidance on making products discoverable in ChatGPT and managing your Shopify storefront.

Updated: 3 days ago

# Information for Shopify merchants

If you use Shopify for your ecommerce platform and want to learn how products can appear in ChatGPT, review Shopify’s guidance on [Agentic Storefronts](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/online-sales-channels/agentic-storefronts).

Shopify’s Help Center includes information about eligibility, Shopify Catalog, product data access, order attribution, and managing Agentic Storefronts in Shopify Admin.

OpenAI Support cannot manage your Shopify Admin, product catalog, storefront settings, order reports, or merchant policies.

For additional information on how products appear to consumers in ChatGPT, see [here](/en/articles/11128490-shopping-with-chatgpt-search). Shopify stores may appear to users when relevant, and users check out on the merchants' online store.
