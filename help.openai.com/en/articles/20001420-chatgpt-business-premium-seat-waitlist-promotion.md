# ChatGPT Business Premium seat waitlist promotion

Receive up to $500 in workspace service credits when you join the Premium seat waitlist and add qualifying seats to your ChatGPT Business workspace.

Updated: 3 days ago

Eligible ChatGPT Business workspace owners can receive $100 in workspace service credits for each qualifying [Premium seat](https://openai.com/index/premium-seats-chatgpt-business/) they add to their workspace, up to five seats or $500 per workspace. The first 10,000 eligible waitlist workspaces will receive credits.

To qualify, you must:

* Join the Premium seat waitlist before August 20.
* Sign up using the email address associated with the ChatGPT Business workspace where you are the owner.
* Add qualifying Premium seats within 30 days after Premium seats launch.
* Meet the other eligibility requirements described below.

Joining the waitlist alone does not guarantee credits.

## Who is eligible for this offer?

This offer is available to eligible ChatGPT Business workspace owners who join the waitlist before the deadline.

You can participate if:

* You already own an existing ChatGPT Business workspace; or
* You intend to [create a new ChatGPT Business workspace](/en/articles/8980713-sign-up-for-chatgpt-business) after launch and will be its workspace [owner](/en/articles/8542216-managing-members-seat-types-and-roles-in-chatgpt-business#managing-chatgpt-business-workspaces).

  + New workspaces created **must have < 200** members to qualify
* The email used to join the waitlist must match a workspace owner account. You must be a workspace owner when a qualifying Premium seat is purchased and when credits are granted; you do not need to be the original workspace creator or sole billing owner. Signing up as an admin or member does not qualify.

Your submission must be within the first 10,000 eligible workspaces to receive credits. The offer is limited to one ChatGPT Business workspace per waitlist owner. If you own more than one workspace, the first workspace where a qualifying Premium seat is purchased becomes the promotional workspace and cannot be changed.

Multiple owners of the same workspace may join the waitlist or purchase qualifying seats, but the workspace can receive credits for no more than five seats in total.

Workspaces using another active promotion, discount, trial, or promotional credit are not eligible.

OpenAI may withhold credits if an account has previously been associated with a ban or does not pass applicable eligibility and abuse checks.

## How do I participate?

* Join the [Premium seat waitlist](https://openai.com/form/business/premium-offer/) before August 20, using the email you use—or will use—as a ChatGPT Business workspace owner. You must use that email to sign into ChatGPT as a workspace owner.
* **Existing workspace owners:** After Premium seats launch, sign in using the same email used to join the waitlist, then add new Premium seats or upgrade existing Standard seats to Premium.
* **New workspace owners:** After Premium seats launch, create a new ChatGPT Business workspace using the same email used to join the waitlist, then purchase Premium seats.
* Add between one and five qualifying Premium seats to your Business workspace within 30 days after launch.
* After eligibility is verified, OpenAI will grant the applicable workspace credits and notify you by email.

All qualifying seats must be added during the 30-day promotional window.

## How many credits can I receive?

You will receive $100 in workspace service credits for each qualifying Premium seat, up to five seats:

* One qualifying seat: $100 in credits
* Three qualifying seats: $300 in credits
* Five or more qualifying seats: $500 in credits

The maximum promotional grant is $500 per workspace.

## Do upgrades from Standard seats count?

Yes. An existing Standard seat upgraded to Premium after launch and within 30 days after launch counts as a qualifying seat.

## What if I own more than one workspace?

The offer is limited to one workspace per waitlist owner. The first workspace where a qualifying Premium seat is purchased becomes the promotional workspace and cannot be changed.

## Can another workspace owner purchase the seats?

Yes. Any owner of the qualifying workspace may purchase the seats. Multiple owners may join the waitlist, but the workspace can receive credits for no more than five seats in total.

## Can I receive a refund after using promotional credits?

If you use promotional credits issued through this offer, the related Premium-seat purchase is not eligible for a refund. Standard refund rules otherwise apply.

## When will I receive the credits?

Credits will be granted after you add and pay for qualifying Premium seats and OpenAI verifies that the promotional requirements have been met.

We will notify you when credits have been granted.

## Where can I see and use the credits?

Workspace owners can review their available credit balance under Workspace settings → Billing.

The credits are added to the eligible ChatGPT Business workspace and can be used for eligible credit-based ChatGPT features.

## Do credits expire?

Yes. Promotional credits expire in accordance with our [Service Credit terms](https://openai.com/policies/service-credit-terms/).

## Can this offer be combined with another promotion?

No. This offer cannot be combined with other promotions, discounts, trials, or promotional credits.

## What happens if I use a different email address?

The email used for the waitlist must match the email associated with the workspace owner account that adds the Premium seats.

If the emails do not match, or if the waitlist participant is not the workspace owner, the workspace will not be eligible for promotional credits.

## Why might my workspace not receive credits?

Common reasons include:

* You did not use the workspace owner email address.
* The waitlist email was not associated with an owner of the qualifying workspace at purchase and grant time.
* You added the Premium seats more than 30 days after launch.
* You were not one of the first 10,000 eligible workspaces.
* The workspace had already reached the five-seat promotional limit.
* The offer was combined with another promotion, discount, trial, or promotional credit.
* The account did not pass applicable eligibility or abuse checks.
