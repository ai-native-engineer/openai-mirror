# Troubleshooting model feature access

Learn how to troubleshoot limits, workspace permissions, and other restrictions on model access.

Updated: 8 days ago

# Why model access may be limited

You may receive a notification that your model access or capabilities have been temporarily limited, or you may see a model or intelligence option that is still shown but disabled. This can occur for several reasons:

* **Selected model is at capacity. Please try a different model:** If you see a message that a model is at capacity, try again later or choose another available model. Check the [OpenAI status page](https://status.openai.com/) for reported service issues. A model may still be temporarily unavailable even if no incident is listed.
* **Workspace permissions:** In a managed workspace, model availability can depend on workspace settings and role-based access controls. A model that is available to another member might not be available to you.
* **Usage limit reached:** If a model or intelligence option is still shown but disabled, read the message shown with it. If it says you reached a limit or shows a reset time, wait until that time and try again, or choose another available option.
* **Potential Terms of Service violation:** Your account may be flagged for behavior that conflicts with our Terms of Service, such as unauthorized or harmful use of the platform.
* **Security concerns:** Suspicious activity, including multiple failed login attempts or access from unknown locations, may prompt a temporary downgrade.
* **Account changes or maintenance:** Recent modifications to your subscription, plan, or payment information can lead to temporary limitations while updates are processed.

# Resolve the issue

If the issue occurs in a managed workspace, first confirm that you’re in the intended workspace and ask a workspace owner to review your access to the affected model. Workspace permissions are separate from usage limits and account restrictions. For details, see: [Role Based Access Controls for ChatGPT Enterprise](https://help.openai.com/articles/11750701).

1. **Wait and try again:** Capacity limits can be temporary. If a model is at capacity, wait a few minutes and retry. If you need to continue sooner, choose another available model.
2. **Review the terms and policies:** Review our [terms and policies](https://openai.com/policies/) to ensure your actions align with our policies. Possible violations include:

   * Sharing account credentials with unauthorized parties.
   * Using our services for harmful or prohibited activities.
   * Attempting to misuse or exploit the platform.
3. **Re-secure your account:** Secure your account, then check whether access returns. If restrictions continue, follow the troubleshooting steps below or contact OpenAI Support.

   * [Reset your password](https://help.openai.com/articles/4936828).
   * [Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)](https://help.openai.com/articles/7967234).
4. **Wait and try again later:** Sometimes, recent account changes, maintenance periods, or ongoing security reviews cause temporary downgrades. Try again later. If access remains limited, contact OpenAI Support.

# Contact OpenAI Support

If you’ve completed the steps above and continue to experience issues, contact OpenAI Support.
