# Claiming ad credits with ChatGPT Business

Check whether your workspace qualifies, link your ad account, and track your credits in Ads Manager.

Updated: yesterday

If you own an eligible ChatGPT Business workspace, you can claim up to $50 USD per month to advertise on ChatGPT. The offer runs through January 31, 2027, while supplies last.

## Before you sign up or claim

New and existing paid Business workspaces can qualify if their billing address is in the United States, the United Kingdom, Germany, or France. Only workspace owners can see the offer in **Benefits**. If you’re a workspace admin or member, ask an owner to claim it.

Each workspace can link one ad account, and each ad account can claim this offer for one workspace. If your ad account has an active signup promotion, you can’t select it until that promotion ends. If you’re considering signing up for Business, check your ad account’s promotions first. You may only find out that your account is ineligible after checkout.

# Claim your ad credits

1. Sign in to ChatGPT on the web and switch to the Business workspace you own.
2. Open your workspace settings and select **Benefits**. Under **Ads credits**, select **Claim ad credits**.
3. Select an ad account you administer, or select **Create a new Ads account**. Whether you link an existing account or create one, you can’t use the standard signup promotion on that account while the Business offer is active.
4. Review the credit amount, expiration date, and offer terms, then select **Link & claim**.

After you claim, select **Go to Ads Manager** to see your balance in your ad account’s billing currency. You can claim without buying ads upfront. Before you run a campaign, complete [billing setup](https://help.openai.com/articles/20001216) in Ads Manager.

# How much credit you receive

Your monthly credit matches your paid Business seat fees after discounts. Your workspace can receive up to $50 USD per month or the equivalent in your ad account’s billing currency. Taxes and usage charges don’t count toward the credit. For example, if you pay $200 in eligible seat fees for a full month, your workspace earns $50 in ad credits.

If you pay monthly, your first credit is prorated for the time left in your paid billing month. Future credits are added on your billing date after payment succeeds. If you pay annually, your credit is spread across monthly installments while your paid subscription and the offer are active. The same monthly cap applies.

Adding paid seats can increase your credit up to the monthly cap. Removing or downgrading seats reduces future credits when the lower fees take effect. Reassigning a seat doesn’t earn extra credit. If you cancel Business, you stop receiving new credits at your next renewal. If you upgrade to Enterprise, new credits stop when the upgrade takes effect.

# Use and track your credits

Each credit expires 30 days after it’s issued. It covers eligible ad spend on your linked account from the date of issue. You’re still responsible for earlier charges and existing invoice balances. Credits have no cash value and can’t be transferred.

Your campaigns can keep spending after you use up your credits. For example, if you have $50 in credits and your ads incur $55 in eligible spend, the credits cover $50 and you’re billed for the remaining $5.

To see how much credit you’ve used, open **Billing > Activity** in Ads Manager and look for **Promotions**. For more about charges and credit restrictions, see [Billing & Payment](https://help.openai.com/articles/20001216) and the [Ad Credit Terms](https://openai.com/policies/ad-credit-terms/).
