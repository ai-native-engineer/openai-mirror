# OpenAI’s TSE Compliance Plan and Submitting Reports about Brazil’s 2026 Elections

This article sets out helpful information on our compliance with Brazil’s TSE.

Updated: 5 days ago

OpenAI is committed to complying with Brazil’s [TSE Ordinance No. 463/2026](https://www.tse.jus.br/legislacao/compilada/prt/2026/portaria-no-463-de-27-de-julho-de-2026), which establishes various requirements for certain internet application providers in connection with the integrity of Brazil’s 2026 General Elections.

## About the TSE Compliance Plan

OpenAI’s [public TSE Compliance Plan](https://cdn.openai.com/trust-and-transparency/plano-de-conformidade-oai-16-de-agosto-de-2026.pdf) describes the safeguards, policies, reporting channels, and response processes relevant to the 2026 Brazilian elections. You can also learn more through our pages on [2026 Election Information and Safeguards](https://openai.com/index/election-safeguards-2026/), [Trust and Transparency](https://openai.com/trust-and-transparency/), and [Transparency and Content Moderation](https://openai.com/pt-BR/transparency-and-content-moderation/).

## Submitting Reports about Elections-related Content

Authorized Brazilian government authorities may submit valid legal process, content-related requests, and other official communications through OpenAI’s dedicated [Kodex portal](https://app.kodexglobal.com/signin). You will need a verified government account. If you do not already have one, create an account through the [Kodex government signup page](https://app.kodexglobal.com/gov/signup) and complete the required verification steps. Please see below for more information on how to sign-up.

If you have trouble creating an account, signing in, or accessing the platform, contact Kodex Support at [help@kodexglobal.com](mailto:help@kodexglobal.com). Kodex is reserved for authorized government authorities.

If you are not an authorized government authority, including candidates, political parties, federations, and coalitions, submit your report through OpenAI’s [content reporting form](https://openai.com/pt-BR/form/report-content/).

For more information on our approach to elections, including the safeguards we deploy, please see our [Elections Information and Safeguards page](https://openai.com/index/election-safeguards-2026/?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

## How to Contact OpenAI via Kodex (for Government Authorities)

OpenAI uses Kodex to receive and respond to communications from government authorities. This page explains how to submit a request or inquiry to OpenAI through Kodex.

### Before you start

You will need a Kodex account. If you do not already have one, create an account on [the Kodex website](https://app.kodexglobal.com/gov/signup) and complete any verification steps requested by Kodex during signup.

If you have trouble creating an account, logging in, or accessing the Kodex platform, please contact Kodex Support: help@kodexglobal.com.

Note: This portal is reserved for authorized government authorities only. If you are not an authorized government authority, please refer to our [Report Content form](https://openai.com/pt-BR/form/report-content/) to tell us about potential policy violations or illegal content.

### How to submit a request to OpenAI

Step 1: Log in and select OpenAI

1. Sign in to your Kodex portal.
2. In the list of organizations available in Kodex, select OpenAI.

Step 2: Choose the relevant submission path

Kodex provides two main submission paths for OpenAI:

##### **1) Content Removal**

Use Content Removal when your request relates to content that you believe should be removed or restricted, including:

* Requests or orders relating to specific content
* Notices that content may be unlawful or otherwise prohibited
* Requests that require content review and action

##### **2) User Data**

Use User Data when your request relates to access to user data. Guidance is found in our [OpenAI Government User Data Request Policy](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/openai-law-enforcement-policy-v.2025-12.pdf).

If you are unsure which path applies, choose the path that best matches your request. You may also use Quick Question (below) to ask for guidance.

### Submitting general questions or compliance inquiries (“Quick Question”)

If your message is not a content removal request and is not a user data request — for example, a general question about compliance, processes, or how to submit a request — use the Quick Question option:

1. Select either Content Removal or User Data (either is acceptable for general inquiries).
2. Locate Quick Question (top-right of the page).
3. Submit your inquiry in the free-form text field and include any relevant details.

### What happens after you submit

* Your submission is delivered to OpenAI for review.
* OpenAI will respond through Kodex.
* You will be notified in Kodex and/or by email associated with your Kodex account when a response is posted.
