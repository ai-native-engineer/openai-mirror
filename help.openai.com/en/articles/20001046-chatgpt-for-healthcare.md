# ChatGPT for Healthcare

Learn how ChatGPT for Healthcare supports clinical work, enterprise security, governance, and HIPAA-compliant use.

## Overview

ChatGPT for Healthcare is an enterprise version of ChatGPT built for clinicians, administrators, and researchers. It helps teams reduce administrative work, support clinical reasoning, and create patient-ready outputs—within a secure workspace designed to support HIPAA compliance.

It combines OpenAI’s latest healthcare-optimized models with enterprise-grade security, governance, and compliance, plus access to trusted clinical search.

## What you can do with it

### Get clinical answers with citations

ChatGPT for Healthcare can pull from millions of peer-reviewed studies, clinical guidelines, and public health sources. Responses include **clear citations** (title, journal, authors, and publication date) so clinicians can verify the evidence or explore the source directly.

The system automatically selects appropriate sources for each question—no database setup or filtering required.

Clinical search is controlled by workspace admins. Admins can enable it at workspace level or use role-based access control (RBAC) to grant access only to specific user groups—such as clinicians or clinical reviewers—rather than turning it on for everyone.

### Work with your organization’s knowledge

ChatGPT for Healthcare can connect to enterprise systems like **Microsoft SharePoint, Teams, and Outlook** so answers reflect your organization’s approved policies, care pathways, and internal documentation. Access is controlled by admins using role-based access controls (RBAC).

Teams can also create and share reusable templates for common workflows, such as documentation, prior authorizations, or patient communications.

### Support day-to-day clinical and operational work

Teams can use ChatGPT for Healthcare to:

* Review approved care pathways
* Synthesize medical evidence with citations
* Reason through differentials
* Draft prior authorization letters
* Summarize patient information
* Create discharge instructions and patient-ready explanations

Final decisions always remain with the clinician.

## Security, privacy, and support for HIPAA compliance

ChatGPT for Healthcare is built to support regulated healthcare use.

### Enterprise security and governance

Organizations get:

* Role-based access controls (RBAC)
* SAML SSO and SCIM
* Audit logs
* Data residency options
* Customer-managed encryption keys

### Support for HIPAA-compliant use

ChatGPT for Healthcare supports HIPAA-compliant use through:

* Enterprise security controls
* No training on data
* Data retention controls
* Availability of a **Business Associate Agreement (BAA)** with OpenAI

## FAQ

### How has GPT-5.2 been evaluated for healthcare use?

GPT-5.2 has been evaluated by licensed physicians across realistic clinical scenarios and benchmarks like HealthBench and GDPval. In real-world healthcare tasks, GPT-5.2 surpasses earlier OpenAI models and performs better than human baselines across every role measured in [GDPval](https://openai.com/index/gdpval/).

### What kinds of teams can use ChatGPT for Healthcare?

Clinical teams, care coordinators, administrators, researchers, and operational staff can all use ChatGPT for Healthcare. Organizations can tailor access and workflows based on role.

### What is the difference between ChatGPT Enterprise and ChatGPT for Healthcare?

ChatGPT for Healthcare is built for regulated healthcare use, and also includes enterprise-grade security, data privacy, and compliance, like role-based access (RBAC), data residency, and audit logs—like ChatGPT Enterprise. ChatGPT for Healthcare includes additional healthcare-specific capabilities not available in ChatGPT Enterprise, such as access to trusted clinical search with citations, which admins can make available to users in their workspace using RBAC.

### How is ChatGPT for Healthcare priced?

Pricing is based on ChatGPT Enterprise and depends on organization size and deployment needs. Enterprise workspaces purchase a shared credit pool at the contract level. All users in the workspace draw from this pool when using advanced features. There are no per-seat usage caps. [Read more about it here](/en/articles/11487671-flexible-pricing-for-the-enterprise-edu-and-business-plans), and contact sales for details.

### Can non-clinical staff use ChatGPT for Healthcare?

Yes. Clinical administrators and support teams can use it to draft forms and letters, summarize information, coordinate care, and standardize documentation—saving time across operational workflows.

### How do organizations get access?

Large hospitals and health systems can [contact the OpenAI sales team](https://openai.com/contact-sales/) to learn more.

### Is customer data used to train OpenAI models?

No. Content shared with ChatGPT for Healthcare is not used to train OpenAI’s models.

### Can I build custom healthcare applications with OpenAI?

Yes. Healthcare organizations and developers can use the [OpenAI API](https://openai.com/api/) to embed AI into their own clinical and operational systems—such as documentation tools, care coordination platforms, and patient workflows—using the latest models (including GPT-5.2).

If you need a BAA for our API services, [learn how to apply](/en/articles/8660679-how-can-i-get-a-business-associate-agreement-baa-with-openai-for-the-api-services). Enterprise API customers can contact their account team to request access.
