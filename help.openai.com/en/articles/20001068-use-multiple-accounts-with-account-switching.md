# Use multiple accounts with account switching

Switch between two ChatGPT accounts while keeping their conversations, billing, workspaces, and settings separate.

Updated: 8 hours ago

> Account switching is currently available on ChatGPT web; it is not yet supported in Codex desktop or the native ChatGPT mobile apps.

Account switching lets you stay signed in to two ChatGPT accounts at the same time and switch between them instantly — no logging out required.

This makes it easy to keep your work and personal usage separate while using the same browser or device.

## What the account switcher does

With the account switcher, you can:

* Be signed into two accounts in the same session
* Switch between them instantly
* Keep chats, memory, history, billing and workspaces fully separate

Each account remains independent. Switching does **not** merge or combine them.

## How the account switcher works

#### Stay signed into 2 accounts

You can be logged into:

* A personal account (e.g., [bobby@gmail.com](mailto:bobby@gmail.com))
* A work account (e.g., [bobby@acmecorp.com](mailto:bobby@acmecorp.com))

Only one account is active at a time, but switching is immediate.

You can have a maximum of two accounts active in the switcher per session. You can still create additional accounts, but you’ll need to log out to access more than two at once.

#### Add an account

To add a second account:

1. Open the account menu
2. Select “Add account”
3. Sign in or create a new account.

You do not need to log out of your current account to add another one.

Creating a second account does **not** require payment. Your newly created account will start on the free plan by default.

#### Switch accounts

Use the account menu to switch accounts. When you switch, all chats, memory, files and settings will reflect the selected account. Workspaces (if applicable) will be scoped to that account.

Switching is **not** merging. Switching does not:

* Move chats between accounts
* Share memory or conversation history
* Share files or uploads
* Combine billing or subscriptions
* Merge workspaces

If you want your work data governed by your organization, make sure you are using your work account before starting a conversation.

## Work and Enterprise accounts

If your organization manages your ChatGPT account:

* Your administrator may be able to access and manage data tied to that account.
* Data created in your managed account is governed by your organization’s policies.
* Switching accounts does not change which policies apply.

You can read more [here](/en/articles/20001067-data-access-for-your-managed-chatgpt-account). If you’re unsure which account you’re using, check the active account shown in the account menu. If you have questions about your organization-managed account, contact your IT administrator.

# Frequently asked questions

#### Can I merge two accounts via account switching?

No. Account switching allows you to access multiple accounts easily, but it does not merge them.

#### Can I use this with all plan types?

Account switching can be used with all plan types, but your organization may control whether account switching is available for your managed workspace. If you don’t see the option to add or switch accounts in a managed workspace, check with your workspace administrator.

#### What happens if my workspace is deactivated?

If a workspace tied to an account is deactivated:

* You may see a banner explaining the change.
* You may be prompted to sign in with a different account or purchase a new subscription (depending on plan type).
* ChatGPT will not automatically switch you to another account.

#### When should I use a second account?

A separate account is useful if you want:

* Clear separation between work and personal chats
* Separate conversation history
* Different billing or subscription plans
* Different workspaces
* Different privacy or administrative controls
* If you want your work activity to stay under your organization’s governance, use your managed work account for work-related conversations.
