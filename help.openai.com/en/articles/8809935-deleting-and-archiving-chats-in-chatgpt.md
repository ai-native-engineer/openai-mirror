# Deleting and archiving chats in ChatGPT

Learn how to delete, archive, and unarchive conversations in ChatGPT.

Updated: 4 days ago

Delete a chat when you no longer need it. Archive a chat when you want to remove it from your regular sidebar without deleting it.

Deleting is permanent. Archiving keeps the conversation in your account under the same retention rules as other saved chats.

# Delete a chat

1. Open your ChatGPT chat history.
2. Select the more options menu (•••) for the chat you want to delete.
3. Select **Delete**.
4. Confirm the deletion.

![ChatGPT chat options menu with Archive and Delete actions for a conversation](https://images.ctfassets.net/j22is2dtoxu1/intercom-img-c5a676f63cbc58934abdd6bd/c259b9d9f5056a4a0eaaf37e688b4469/image.png?q=80&fm=webp&w=714)

When you delete a chat:

* ChatGPT removes it from your account view immediately.
* OpenAI schedules it for permanent deletion from its systems within 30 days, unless it was already de-identified and disassociated from your account or OpenAI must retain it longer for security or legal obligations.
* You cannot restore a deleted chat in ChatGPT or through OpenAI Support. Authorized compliance tools may still access eligible workspace data under your organization’s retention policy.

# Archive a chat

1. Open your ChatGPT chat history.
2. Select the more options menu (•••) for the chat you want to archive.
3. Select **Archive**.

No confirmation is required.

![ChatGPT chat options menu with Archive selected for a conversation](https://images.ctfassets.net/j22is2dtoxu1/intercom-img-3b8022bffe4081877a61edf5/7a00c36992e4cbebf9a4d4a29065873c/image.png?q=80&fm=webp&w=656)

Archiving removes the chat from the regular sidebar but does not delete it. Archived chats remain searchable.

# Manage archived chats

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **Data controls**.
3. Select **Archived chats** or **View archived chats**, depending on your device. If **Manage** appears, select it.
4. Select **Unarchive** to return a chat to regular history, or select **Delete** to delete it.

![Archived Chats list with an Unarchive conversation tooltip for a saved chat](https://images.ctfassets.net/j22is2dtoxu1/intercom-img-976cd676294a366e12047002/fd6c06838929e2d94b2fb0dbd734f422/image.png?q=80&fm=webp&w=1344)

# Archive or delete all chats

ChatGPT includes controls to archive or delete chats in bulk.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **Data controls**.
3. Select **Archive all chats** or **Delete all chats**.
4. Review the confirmation message, then continue.

![ChatGPT settings options to Archive all chats or Delete all chats](https://images.ctfassets.net/j22is2dtoxu1/intercom-img-01ed15d6ba88f6c7f761b8dc/e820362ee07d266bc0c02340f5791b3a/image.png?q=80&fm=webp&w=812)

These actions can affect chats in your current account or workspace, including chats in projects. The **Delete all chats** action cannot be undone.

# Chat retention

* Regular and archived chats remain saved to your account until you delete them or an applicable workspace retention policy removes them.
* Archiving a chat does not change its retention period.
* Deleted chats are removed from your account view immediately and scheduled for permanent deletion within 30 days, subject to the exceptions described above.

For more information about chat retention, see: [Chat and file retention in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/articles/8983778).

Deleting a chat does not delete files saved separately in Library. Delete the files from Library if you also want to remove those saved copies.

# Connected app data

If a conversation used data from a connected app, archiving the chat keeps the conversation and any connected-app data retained within it.

Deleting the chat removes the conversation and connected-app data retained within that conversation from your account, subject to standard retention and legal obligations.

Deleting a chat does not delete the original data from the connected app. Disconnecting an app prevents future access but does not delete past conversations that already used app data.

# Troubleshooting missing chats

If a conversation is missing:

* Confirm that you are signed in to the correct OpenAI account and workspace, including the correct email address or single sign-on (SSO) method.
* Use **Search** to look for the conversation.
* Go to **Settings > Data controls**, then select **Archived chats** or **View archived chats**, depending on your device.
* Refresh ChatGPT, or sign out and sign in again.
* Check OpenAI Status for an active incident.
* Request a data export if you need to review data currently associated with an eligible account.

Deleted chats, including chats removed with **Delete all chats**, cannot be restored.

When contacting OpenAI Support, include your account email and sign-in method, workspace name when applicable, the approximate date of the missing conversations, and the checks you completed.
