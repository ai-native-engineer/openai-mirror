# Finding past ChatGPT invoices

Find past ChatGPT Plus, Pro, or Business invoices in your billing settings.

Updated: 2 days ago

You can find past invoices for ChatGPT subscriptions purchased on the web in your billing settings. The steps differ for Go, Plus, or Pro subscriptions and Business workspaces.

For subscriptions purchased through the Apple App Store or Google Play, use the store receipt instructions below.

# Go, Plus, or Pro

1. In ChatGPT on the web, open [**Settings > Billing**](https://chatgpt.com/#settings/Billing) and find **Invoices**.
2. In the **Invoices** section, select **View** beside an invoice to open it. Select **View all** to see more invoices.

## Troubleshooting

* If your **Invoices** section is missing or empty, make sure you’re signed into the correct ChatGPT account or workspace. Receipts for subscriptions started through the Apple App Store or Google Play won’t appear here—check your app-store purchase history instead.
* If the billing controls needed to view your invoices are missing, sign in to the same ChatGPT account in a different browser, then try again. If you bought your subscription on the web and the invoice is still missing, [contact Support](https://help.openai.com/articles/6614161) with the billing period and the invoice number, if you have it.
* If an invoice you found needs a correction, see [how to request a review of an issued invoice](https://help.openai.com/articles/9038389).

# Business

From your [workspace settings](https://chatgpt.com/admin/billing), select **Billing**, then **Invoices** to view invoices available for that workspace/account. If none appear, confirm you are an owner on the correct workspace/account and that invoice records exist. If you still can’t locate invoices, contact OpenAI Support with your account email.

## Find invoices for a deactivated workspace

If you are a workspace owner and cannot open **Billing**, [contact Support](https://help.openai.com/articles/6614161) for help locating past invoices. Include your account email, workspace name or ID if known, the billing periods you need, and any previous invoice number. Tell us whether the workspace is deactivated and which billing page you cannot access.

# Apple App Store receipts

Use the Apple Account that paid for your subscription. Its email address may differ from your ChatGPT email. Apple sends an email receipt to that account each time your ChatGPT subscription renews.

## Use your iPhone or iPad

1. Open the **App Store** and select your photo or initials. If prompted, select **My Account** and sign in.
2. Select **Apps & Purchase History**.
3. Select **Purchase History**.
4. Find your ChatGPT purchase. Change the date filter if you need to find an older charge.

## Use a computer or browser

Open [Apple’s purchase history](https://reportaproblem.apple.com), sign in to the Apple Account used for the purchase, and find the ChatGPT charge. If it is missing, check any other Apple Account you may have used. See [Apple’s purchase history instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118212) for other device options.

# Google Play receipts

Use the Google account that paid for your subscription. Google sends purchase confirmations to that account’s email address.

1. Open the **Google Play Store** app.
2. Select your profile icon.
3. Select **Payments & subscriptions**.
4. Select **Budget & history** and find your ChatGPT order.

You can also check purchases on the web. See [Google Play’s order history instructions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2850369?hl=en).
