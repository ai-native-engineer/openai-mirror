# ChatGPT with Slack and Microsoft Teams

Understand how to use ChatGPT with Slack or Microsoft Teams and find the setup guide for your task.

You can work with ChatGPT directly in Slack or Microsoft Teams, or connect these apps to use them while working in ChatGPT. Each experience has its own setup and access settings.

| Where you want to work | What you can do | How to get started |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slack or Microsoft Teams | Ask @ChatGPT questions, summarize information, or draft work in a conversation. | Your administrator sets up @ChatGPT for your organization. |
| ChatGPT | Use connected Slack or Microsoft Teams apps during a ChatGPT conversation. | Connect the app in ChatGPT, subject to your organization’s settings. |

Availability depends on the experience, your plan, and your organization’s settings.

@ChatGPT is available globally to Business and Enterprise users.

# Use @ChatGPT in Slack or Microsoft Teams

Mention @ChatGPT in a supported conversation to ask for help. For example, you can ask it to draft a project update using the discussion in the thread.

Your administrator configures @ChatGPT’s instructions and connections to company tools. It uses the conversation context available to it and those configured tools to respond.

@ChatGPT runs through an account configured for the assistant. Your Slack or Teams account identifies you as the person making the request. Your administrator may require you to connect your ChatGPT account before using it.

Keep these access differences in mind:

* Your personal ChatGPT conversation history, saved memories, and custom instructions do not automatically carry over.
* @ChatGPT does not automatically inherit your access to files, apps, or private channels. Some requests require separate authorization to use apps connected to your own account.
* Replies in shared conversations are visible to the participants. Consider that audience when sharing information or authorizing a request.

To start a conversation and understand account connections, permissions, and privacy, see: [Using @ChatGPT in Slack and Microsoft Teams](https://help.openai.com/articles/20001537).

To set up @ChatGPT for your organization, see: [Setting up and managing @ChatGPT in Slack and Microsoft Teams](https://help.openai.com/articles/20001538).

# Use connected Slack or Microsoft Teams apps in ChatGPT

Connect Slack or Microsoft Teams to work with the app from a ChatGPT conversation. What ChatGPT can access or do depends on the app’s supported capabilities, the connected account’s permissions, and your organization’s settings.

For this experience, you work in ChatGPT using the account connected to the app. Connecting Slack or Microsoft Teams in ChatGPT does not, by itself, install or enable @ChatGPT in your organization’s Slack or Teams conversations.

To connect an app account, manage permissions, or disconnect it, see: [Connecting and managing app accounts in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/articles/20001494).

For Slack capabilities and requirements, see: [Using Slack in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/articles/12525822).

For Microsoft Teams capabilities and requirements, see: [Using Microsoft Teams in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/articles/12552368).

# Existing users of the ChatGPT app in Slack

@ChatGPT replaces the previous ChatGPT app experience in Slack, which lets you use your personal ChatGPT account from Slack’s sidebar.

Existing installations do not stop working immediately when @ChatGPT launches. Moving to the new experience requires migration guidance.

To use the new @ChatGPT in Business or Enterprise accounts, admins must upgrade their Slack app installation to support the additional scopes required for @ChatGPT to function.

Have a Slack admin navigate to the [App details page](https://slack.com/marketplace/A097V82EGG2-chatgpt) for the ChatGPT app within the Slack admin dashboard. Approve the shown scope upgrade request.

For further setup, see: [Setting up and managing @ChatGPT in Slack and Microsoft Teams](https://help.openai.com/articles/20001538).
