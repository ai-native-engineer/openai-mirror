# How do I avoid being charged twice if I subscribe to ChatGPT on iOS, Android, and the web?

Prevent duplicate ChatGPT subscription charges across the Apple App Store, Google Play, and chatgpt.com.

Updated: 8 hours ago

## Overview

If you subscribe to ChatGPT on more than one platform (Apple App Store, Google Play Store, or chatgpt.com), you may end up with multiple active subscriptions and multiple charges. This article explains how to prevent that and what to do if it happens.

## Why duplicate subscriptions happen

Your ChatGPT subscription is managed by the platform where you purchased it:

* **Apple App Store** subscriptions are managed by Apple
* **Google Play Store** subscriptions are managed by Google
* **chatgpt.com** subscriptions are managed on chatgpt.com

If you start a new subscription on one platform before canceling the old one on another platform, you can end up paying for more than one subscription at the same time.

### Recommended option: subscribe on chatgpt.com

If you use ChatGPT across multiple devices, subscribing on **chatgpt.com** is often the easiest option because you can manage your subscription from the web instead of through Apple or Google.

**Important:** If you previously subscribed through Apple or Google, you should **cancel that subscription first** before starting a new one on chatgpt.com.

## Avoiding duplicate subscriptions

To avoid creating a second subscription, you need to find out which platform is currently managing your billing. Start by checking each place where a ChatGPT subscription can exist so you know which plan to keep and which one to cancel, following the steps below:

### iOS (Apple App Store)

1. Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone
2. Tap your **name**
3. Tap **Subscriptions**
4. Look for **ChatGPT**

### Android (Google Play Store)

1. Open the **Google Play Store**
2. Make sure you are signed into the Google account you used to subscribe
3. Go to **Menu > Subscriptions**
4. Look for **ChatGPT**

### ChatGPT Web (chatgpt.com)

1. Log in to ChatGPT on the web
2. Go to **Settings**
3. Open the **Account** section (or **Billing** or **Plan management**)
4. Review whether you have an active plan managed on chatgpt.com

If you have an existing subscription on one of the platforms, and want to change it to another platform (for example, moving from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store to **chatgpt.com,** you must first **cancel your subscription on the same platform where you originally started it.** See steps below to cancel:

### If you subscribed on iOS (Apple App Store)

If you subscribed through Apple, Apple manages your subscription and you must cancel it in iOS settings:

1. Open the **Settings** app on your iPhone
2. Tap your **name**
3. Tap **Subscriptions**
4. Select **ChatGPT**
5. Tap **Cancel Subscription**

### If you subscribed on Android (Google Play Store)

1. Open the **Google Play Store**
2. Make sure you are signed into the Google account you used to subscribe
3. Go to **Menu > Subscriptions**
4. Select **ChatGPT**
5. Tap **Cancel subscription** and follow the prompts

*Note that uninstalling the ChatGPT app does* ***not*** *cancel your Google Play or Apple App Store subscription.*

### If you subscribed on chatgpt.com

1. Log in to ChatGPT on the web
2. Click your profile icon and select **Settings**
3. Go to the **Account** tab
4. Select **Manage**
5. Select **Cancel subscription**

## Duplicate subscriptions due to failed payments on iOS

If your Apple renewal fails because of a payment issue, your subscription is not automatically canceled. Your subscription may appear inactive in ChatGPT, but Apple may continue trying to recover it.

If you start a new subscription on chatgpt.com or the Google Play Store, but later update your Apple payment method in response to the payment issue, Apple may use the new payment method to recover and renew your ChatGPT subscription, which can result in duplicate subscriptions and multiple charges.

To avoid this situation, either:

* Update your Apple payment method and allow Apple to recover your subscription **without starting a new subscription** on a different platform, or
* Cancel your Apple subscription first, even if it looks inactive. After Apple confirms it will not renew, subscribe on chatgpt.com.

## What to do if you were charged twice

First, use the steps above per platform to confirm where you have active subscriptions. Decide which subscription you want to continue, and then request refunds from the platform for the duplicate charge.

* If you subscribed through the **Apple App Store**, request a refund from Apple.
* If you subscribed through **Google Play**, follow Google’s refund process.
* If you subscribed on **chatgpt.com**, or are not sure where you subscribed, [contact support](/en/articles/6614161-how-can-i-contact-support).
