# Communicating with OpenAI support

Learn about our policy on abusive behavior towards OpenAI support teams.

Updated: 14 days ago

At OpenAI, we care about developing and distributing advanced AI models for the benefit of everyone. We understand that these models can be at the core of your business, or form part of your day-to-day life, meaning any disruption can be frustrating.

While we’re here to support you, we also need to maintain a safe working environment and we do not tolerate abusive behavior via our support channels. Abusive behavior includes but is not limited to disparaging or derogatory language, threats of violence to people or property, repeated unwelcome conduct, or other similar actions.

To ensure a safe and constructive space for everyone, users who engage in abusive or threatening conduct may face actions including but not limited to:

* Written or verbal warnings
* Restricted or loss of access to support services
* Temporary or permanent loss of access to OpenAI products and services
* Reporting credible threats of harm to authorities

We are committed to providing excellent support and addressing any questions or concerns you may have. Continued use of harassing or disrespectful language may result in limitations to your access to our support services and, potentially, to your account.
