# ChatGPT Business - Overview

Learn what ChatGPT Business is, how different seat types work, and how pricing, privacy, and admin controls work.

Updated: 10 hours ago

Introducing Premium seats for ChatGPT Business. Premium seats cost $100 per user per month when billed annually, or $125 per user per month when billed monthly. Premium includes 5x more usage than Standard seats, no 5-hour usage limit, and the flexibility to mix, assign, and reassign seat types – all within one secure, centrally managed workspace.

Additionally, we’ve updated the way seats are used in ChatGPT Business. You can purchase Standard or Premium seats to allocate capacity for your workspace, and then assign them to different users, as needed. You can also switch users across seats, add capacity, or remove capacity.

# Overview

ChatGPT Business is a self-serve plan designed for organizations that want a shared ChatGPT workspace for their teams.

A Business workspace provides:

• A secure environment for collaboration
• Generous access to frontier models
• Centralized billing across users
• Admin controls for managing users, roles, and access
• Usage visibility and spend controls
• Access to Codex and ChatGPT, depending on seat type

ChatGPT Business is built around two types of seats: Standard ChatGPT seats and Premium ChatGPT seats. Each seat type determines what usage limits a user has.

| **Seat type** | **Price per seat** | **Included access** | **Billing model** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard seat | Monthly plan: $25 per user per month  Annual plan: $20 per user per month, billed annually | ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex | Fixed per-user cost, billed monthly or annually.  You can also use [flexible pricing](https://help.openai.com/articles/11487671) to purchase workspace credits that extend usage beyond the included rate limits. |
| Premium seat | Monthly plan: $125 per user per month  Annual plan: $100 per user per month, billed annually | ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex **with higher usage limits** | Fixed per-user cost, billed monthly or annually.  You can also use [flexible pricing](https://help.openai.com/articles/11487671) to purchase workspace credits that extend usage beyond the included rate limits. |

**A seat is paid capacity in a workspace,** separate from the member who uses it. Workspace owners purchase seats and can then assign them to new members when inviting them or to existing members. A Business workspace requires at least 2 Standard or Premium seats in total: 2 of either type, or 1 of each.

# Standard ChatGPT seats

Standard ChatGPT seats include access to ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex, as well as features such as GPTs, Projects, Apps, Company Knowledge, ChatGPT Agent, Deep Research, as well as Codex. The seats include baseline access, and you can use [flexible pricing](https://help.openai.com/articles/11487671) and purchase workspace credits for usage beyond the included rate limits.

For most countries, pricing (USD) is $25 per user per month if billed monthly and $20 per user per month if billed annually. Note that pricing may vary depending on your country and currency type - see the pricing [page fo](https://chatgpt.com/pricing)r more details.

Standard ChatGPT seats include access to both ChatGPT Work and Codex, our agentic work product and coding platform. Review included rates and limits, and the [ChatGPT Rate Card](https://help.openai.com/articles/11481834) for prices for usage beyond included limits.

# Premium ChatGPT seats

Premium ChatGPT seats include all the same access as Standard seats, but with [higher usage limits](https://help.openai.com/articles/12003714). You can also use [flexible pricing](https://help.openai.com/articles/11487671) and purchase workspace credits for usage beyond the included rate limits.

# Mixing seat types in a workspace

A Business workspace can include Standard ChatGPT seats, Premium ChatGPT seats, or a mix of both. You can manage seats and change seat types in workspace settings.

If your ChatGPT Business workspace has access to the legacy Codex seat type, you can also include them with standard and premium seats. To learn more, review our article [**Managing legacy Codex seats in ChatGPT Business**](https://help.openai.com/articles/20001492)[.](https://help.openai.com/articles/20001492)

# Privacy, terms, and data handling

ChatGPT Business adheres to our [Business Terms](https://openai.com/policies/business-terms) and the privacy policies listed on the [Enterprise Privacy page](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy). OpenAI won’t train on your workspace’s data.

# ChatGPT Business compared with other plans

ChatGPT Business is a self-serve workspace plan for teams. If you need a larger deployment or more advanced controls, consider [ChatGPT Enterprise](https://help.openai.com/articles/8265053).

ChatGPT Business is separate from the [API platform](https://openai.com/api/). A ChatGPT Business subscription does not include API usage - API usage is billed separately. Learn more about the [API Platform](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs).

If you need invoice billing, purchase orders, bank transfer, wire, ACH, net terms, split payments, Zero Data Retention, BAAs, or other sales-led options, use a contracted offering instead of self-serve ChatGPT Business.

## **FAQ**

## **Is ChatGPT Business the same as ChatGPT Team?**

Yes. ChatGPT Team was renamed to ChatGPT Business on August 29, 2025. The rename itself did not change the plan, but later pricing and seat options can still change over time.

## **Can a single workspace include both Standard ChatGPT seats and Premium seats?**

Yes.

## **Does ChatGPT Business still require 2 users?**

Yes, ChatGPT Business workspaces require 2 Standard or Premium ChatGPT seats (1 of each, for a total of two, meets this criteria as well).

## **Can I use the ChatGPT API with this plan?**

API usage is separate and billed independently.

## **Can I create a new ChatGPT Business account after deleting my previous one?**

Yes. After 30 days, you can create a new account with the same email if the prior account was fully deleted and not merely deactivated. Deleted accounts cannot be reactivated.
