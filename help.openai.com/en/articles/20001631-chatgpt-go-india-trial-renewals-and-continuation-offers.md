# ChatGPT Go India trial renewals and continuation offers

Learn what happens when your India ChatGPT Go trial ends and how to review a continuation offer.

Updated: yesterday

Your free year of ChatGPT Go ends on the date shown in your subscription. If renewal is on, Go continues as a paid monthly subscription. The standard price is ₹399 per month. If you don’t want to continue, [cancel through the provider where you subscribed](https://help.openai.com/articles/7232927) at least 24 hours before your renewal date. If a payment is already processing, it may still go through. If you’ve already been charged and want a refund, follow the [refund request steps](https://help.openai.com/articles/7232895).

A continuation offer may be available for your account. The continuation offers described here are available on the web and in the ChatGPT Android app. They are not available in the ChatGPT iOS app. Offers are optional, and receiving the original trial does not guarantee a continuation offer. Review the price, billing dates, and terms shown for your account before accepting.

# Check your renewal terms

Review your subscription with the provider you used to subscribe: ChatGPT, Google Play, or Apple. Check the trial end date, whether renewal is enabled, and the amount of your next payment.

If you do not accept a continuation offer, your subscription renews at the price shown by your billing provider.

For information about managing Go and viewing invoices, see: [What is ChatGPT Go?](https://help.openai.com/articles/11989085)

# Review a continuation offer

If an offer is available, it may appear when you cancel or return to Go. You can accept the offer shown or continue canceling. You do not need a promo code for an offer shown in this flow.

The continuation offer applies the displayed monthly price for 3 paid billing periods. After those periods, the subscription renews at ₹399 per month unless you cancel. The offer is a paid subscription discount; it does not restart the original free year.

Before accepting, check:

* The monthly price and the date of the first payment.
* The 3 paid billing periods covered by the offer.
* The price and billing date after the offer ends.

Accepting an offer while your original trial or current paid period is still active preserves the remaining time in that period. The discounted paid periods start at the next renewal. Check the confirmation and subscription details after accepting.

Offers can differ by account and subscription provider. An offer shown to someone else may not be available to you. A previously used continuation offer cannot be restarted.

# Return after canceling

If you canceled but still have Go access, your current subscription has not yet ended. If you accept an available continuation offer, renewal resumes on that subscription. Review its next billing date and price.

If your subscription has ended and your account is on Free, returning to Go requires a new checkout. Review any available offer and the payment terms before subscribing. Returning does not restore the expired free year.

Apple manages subscriptions purchased through the Apple App Store. Follow the renewal and cancellation options shown for that subscription.

# Resolve payment or offer problems

If a payment fails, check your current subscription status and the payment instructions from your billing provider. Go access and the steps to continue depend on the subscription's current state. A failed payment does not guarantee a continuation offer.

If you paid for Go and lost access before the end date on your receipt, contact Support before buying Go again so we can check your paid access.

If an offer shown to you fails when you try to accept it, or an accepted offer does not match your subscription details or charge, [contact OpenAI Support](https://help.openai.com/articles/6614161). Include:

* The email address for the ChatGPT account you used.
* Where you subscribed: ChatGPT, Google Play, or Apple.
* The offer price and terms you saw, plus the date, time, and time zone of your acceptance attempt or error.
* A screenshot of the offer or error and any relevant receipt, with sensitive payment details hidden.

Do not share your full payment card number, password, or one-time verification codes.

# Cancel or request a refund

To stop future renewals, cancel through the provider where you subscribed. Cancel at least 24 hours before your next billing date. For provider-specific steps, see: [Canceling your ChatGPT subscription](https://help.openai.com/articles/7232927).

Canceling does not automatically refund previous charges. For eligibility and the correct request process, see: [How do I request a refund for my ChatGPT subscription?](https://help.openai.com/articles/7232895)
