# Using multiple ChatGPT accounts on web and mobile

Switch between ChatGPT accounts while keeping their conversations, billing, workspaces, and settings separate.

Updated: 2 days ago

*Account switching is rolling out on iOS and Android. If you don’t see* ***Switch account*** *in* ***Settings****, it may not be available to you yet.*

Account switching lets you stay signed in to up to 2 separate ChatGPT accounts at a time and switch between them without signing out, such as a personal account and a work account. Only one account is active at a time. Each account keeps its own chats, files, memory, settings, and subscriptions.

## Availability

Account switching is available on ChatGPT web and in the ChatGPT iOS and Android apps.

On the web, account switching can be used with all plan types. For Enterprise accounts, your workspace owner can allow or block account switching on the web and in the iOS and Android apps where it is available. If account switching is unavailable for your Enterprise account, contact your workspace owner.

# How the account switcher works

## Stay signed in to up to 2 accounts

You can stay signed in to up to 2 accounts at a time. You can have more than 2 ChatGPT accounts. If 2 accounts are already signed in, log out of one before adding another.

Creating a second account does **not** require payment. Your newly created account will start on the Free plan by default.

## Add an account on the web

1. Open the account menu.
2. Select **Add account**.
3. Sign in or create a new account.

If 2 accounts are already signed in, select **Add another account**. Choose an account to log out of, then select **Log out and continue** to sign in to the account you want to add.

## Add an account on iOS or Android

If 2 accounts are already signed in, **Add account** is hidden. Log out of one account to make **Add account** available again.

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **Switch account**.
3. Select **Add account**.
4. Sign in to the account you want to add.

## Switch accounts

On the web, open the account menu and select the account you want to use. On iOS or Android, open **Settings**, select **Switch account**, and select the other account.

When you switch, chats, memory, files, and settings reflect the selected account. Workspaces, where available, are scoped to that account. Check the active account before starting a conversation or making a purchase.

Switching does not:

* Move chats between accounts
* Share memory or conversation history
* Share files or uploads
* Combine billing or subscriptions
* Merge accounts or workspaces

# Notifications on mobile

You can receive notifications for both signed-in accounts, depending on each account’s settings and your device permissions. Selecting a notification for the other account switches to that account and opens the relevant content.

# Log out of an account

Logging out removes that account from the switcher.

On mobile, logging out of the active account opens the other signed-in account. Logging out of the last account returns you to the sign-in screen.

## Log out on the web

1. Open the account menu.
2. Select **Log out**.
3. If 2 accounts are signed in, select the account you want to log out of.
4. Confirm if prompted.

## Log out on iOS or Android

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **Switch account**.
3. Open the more options menu (•••) for the account you want to log out of.
4. Select **Log out** and confirm.

# Subscriptions and purchases

Each account keeps its own plan. Switching accounts does not transfer or share a subscription.

If you already paid but cannot access your subscription, check the ChatGPT account that owns it before making another purchase.

If you want a paid plan on another ChatGPT account, you need a separate subscription. Check for existing subscriptions before purchasing to avoid duplicate charges.

For help recovering a subscription, purchasing a new one, changing or canceling a plan, or resolving a purchase error, see: [Troubleshooting ChatGPT subscription access and account changes](https://help.openai.com/articles/20001056).

# Workspace-managed accounts

For an Enterprise workspace, a workspace owner can allow or block account switching in [Admin Console](https://admin.openai.com/). Select the workspace, open **Workspace settings**, and turn **Enable account switching** on or off. This setting applies on the web and in the iOS and Android apps where account switching is available.

If your organization manages your ChatGPT account:

* Your administrator may be able to access and manage data tied to that account.
* Data created in your managed account is governed by your organization’s policies.
* Switching accounts does not change which policies apply.

Read more about [data access for your managed ChatGPT account](https://help.openai.com/articles/20001067). If you’re unsure which account you’re using, check the active account shown in the account menu. If you have questions about your organization-managed account, contact your IT administrator.

# Frequently asked questions

## Is switching accounts the same as switching workspaces?

No. Switching accounts changes which ChatGPT account you are using. Switching workspaces changes the workspace within an account.

## What happens if my workspace is deactivated?

On the web, if a workspace tied to an account is deactivated:

* You may see a banner explaining the change.
* You may be prompted to sign in with a different account or purchase a new subscription, depending on the plan.
* ChatGPT will not automatically switch you to another account.
