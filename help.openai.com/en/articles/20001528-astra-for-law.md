# Astra for Law

Learn about Astra for Law’s legal research capabilities, access requirements, and legal plugins.

Updated: 10 days ago

Astra for Law is GPT-6 Astra tailored for professional legal work. It combines a dedicated Legal Search Index with custom instructions for legal analysis and writing. It can help lawyers research legal questions based on client facts, develop arguments and deal terms, and consider the strongest objections.

Astra for Law uses the Legal Search Index to search U.S. case law, statutes, regulations, court rules, and administrative decisions. The index is updated with new sources daily. You can use the model to examine cases that weaken an argument or understand how a contract exception changes who bears the risk. Review the answers and cited sources before relying on them.

# Request access

To request early access, contact your OpenAI account team or [OpenAI Sales](https://openai.com/contact-sales/).

Astra for Law will initially be offered to selected law firms in the United States through Trusted Access, Codex, and will be coming soon to the API. Access is for eligible lawyers and people working under their supervision.

The model will appear as **GPT-6 Astra Law** in the model picker.

# Explore legal plugins

Partner-built plugins bring tools and knowledge your firm already uses into ChatGPT. For example, with iManage, a lawyer can draft a negotiation brief in ChatGPT and save it to the matter file, or use DeepJudge to bring prior deals into a comparison.

Community plugins include custom skills built by lawyers and legal engineers at LegalQuants, LECG, and Skills.law. You can adapt these skills for your legal work in ChatGPT.

See [Plugins in ChatGPT and Codex](https://help.openai.com/articles/20001256) for instructions on finding and installing plugins. Check each plugin’s listing for availability and access requirements.
