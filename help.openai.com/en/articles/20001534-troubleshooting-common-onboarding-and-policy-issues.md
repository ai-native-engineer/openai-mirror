# Troubleshooting Common Onboarding and Policy issues

A guide to resolving common onboarding and policy issues related to account or ad review in Ads Manager Beta.

Updated: 20 hours ago

Use this guide to troubleshoot common issues in Ads Manager Beta. Start with the issue category that best matches your issue, work through the recommended checks, and contact support if the issue persists. Account verification, account approval, ad review, and ad delivery are separate checks. Completing one does not mean all the others are complete.

## 1. Business verification

### My business verification failed

* **What it means:** We have completed the verification of your account and were unable to approve it. Verification can fail when we could not verify the accuracy of your business identity, ownership, or affiliations.
* **Next step:** Please ensure your account, ads, and landing pages accurately represent your business and any relationships with other individuals, organizations, or brands.

### My business verification in progress

* **What it means:** Your account verification is in progress and no action is required from you at this time.
* **Next step:** The review may take up to 7 Business days to complete, please reach out to Ads Support if it takes longer.

### My business verification not Started

* **Check first:** Return to the verification prompt in the correct Ads Manager account. Refresh the page or sign in again using an up-to-date browser.
* **Next step:** If it still does not allow you to proceed, contact Ads Support with the failed step.

## 2. Industry eligibility

### My business or industry is not eligible to advertise

* **What it means:** The Ads Policies currently prohibit your industry or services from advertising with OpenAI. Please refer to the [Eligibility table](#ads-policy-eligibility-table-for-highly-regulated-areas) below for the allowed industry and services category. A registered or licensed business is not automatically eligible for every advertising category or market.
* **Next step:** You may have received an email with details on the rejection reason and next steps. If you think the decision is incorrect, please appeal using the link in that email or via Ads Manager. Appeal is the fastest way for resolution, if there are any issues in raising an appeal or you do not see the appeal option, please contact support.

## 3. Ad Rejections ([Policy Eligibility table](#ads-policy-eligibility-table-for-highly-regulated-areas))

### My ad was rejected

* **Check first:** Read the rejection message in the Ads Manager and identify the affected text, image, or landing page. Check that the complete ad and destination comply with the [Ads Policies](https://openai.com/policies/ad-policies/) and describe the same offering.
* **Next step:** If you believe that the policy reason does not apply to your ad, you may use the appeal option in Ads Manager or the Request Review button. Appeal is the fastest path for resolution so reach out to Ads Support only if no appeal option is available or the appeal is pending for more than 7 days without decision.

### My ads cannot serve in the countries I selected

* **What it means:** Compare the campaign's target countries with the allowed policy categories. This error message shows up when the policy categories are not allowed in specific geographies. Certain categories are restricted to select approved advertisers only. At this time we are only working with a small group of advertisers and are not accepting applications for approval in certain policy categories. Refer to the Ad Policy Eligibility table below.
* **Next step:** If you feel the policy restriction on the ad is incorrect, use the appeal option in Ads Manager or the rejection notice when available. Appeal is the fastest path for resolution so reach out to Ads Support only if no appeal option is available or the appeal is pending for more than 7 days without decision.

## 4. Domain Approvals

### My domain is not approved for this account

* **What it means:** We could not verify that your advertiser identity matches your provided domain.
* **Next step:** Please verify that your advertiser identity matches the domain information provided. You must have received an email with details on the rejection reason and next steps. If you think the decision is incorrect, please appeal using the link in that email. Appeal is the quickest way for resolution, if there are any issues in raising an appeal or you do not see the appeal option, please contact Ads Support.

Ads Support can be reached at ads-support@openai.com. In your outreach, please include your ad account id and the category of issue you are experiencing.
