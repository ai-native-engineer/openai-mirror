# Using ChatGPT in CarPlay

Use ChatGPT in Apple CarPlay to start or resume voice conversations while you drive.

Updated: 9 hours ago

ChatGPT is available in Apple CarPlay on supported iPhone models running iOS 26.4 or later. Once your iPhone and the ChatGPT app are up to date, you can open ChatGPT from your CarPlay screen and start a voice conversation while you drive. ChatGPT in CarPlay is available globally on all ChatGPT plans.

ChatGPT in CarPlay is designed to be voice-first. You can start a new conversation, or continue a recent chat or [project](/en/articles/10169521-projects-in-chatgpt) from your car screen. Some features are not available in CarPlay. [Learn more about voice mode in ChatGPT.](/en/articles/8400625-voice-mode-faq)

Please ensure that you have the following before you use ChatGPT in CarPlay:

* a supported iPhone running iOS 26.4 or later
* the latest version of the ChatGPT app
* a car that supports Apple CarPlay

## Start a voice chat

1. Connect your iPhone to your car to start CarPlay.
2. In CarPlay, select **ChatGPT**.
3. Select **New voice chat**.
4. Wait for the connection to establish, and the text to go from Connecting to Listening.
5. Speak your prompt to begin the conversation.

## Start a voice chat in a project

1. In CarPlay, select **ChatGPT**.
2. Select the **Projects** tab.
3. Select the project you want to start a new chat within.
4. Select **New voice chat**.

## Resume a chat

1. In CarPlay, select **ChatGPT**.
2. Select a pinned or recent chat you want to continue.
3. Speak your prompt to continue the conversation.

## For your safety

Only use your mobile device when allowed by law and when conditions permit safe use. Set up the app before driving, rely on hands-free, voice-first features whenever possible, and avoid interacting with your device while the vehicle is in motion.

# FAQ

## Can I open ChatGPT in CarPlay directly in voice mode?

Yes. ChatGPT in CarPlay opens to the Chats screen by default. You can skip this and start in voice mode directly:

1. Connect your iPhone to your car to start CarPlay.
2. In CarPlay, select ChatGPT.
3. **On your iPhone**, open the ChatGPT app, and navigate to Settings.
4. Under Voice, enable “Start automatically in CarPlay”.

The next time you open ChatGPT in CarPlay, it will open directly in voice mode.

## Why did ChatGPT reopen when I got back in my car?

CarPlay by default reopens the last app you used when your phone reconnects. If the last app was ChatGPT, it may reopen automatically.

## Why does voice quality vary by car?

Audio input and output depend on your car’s microphones, speakers, and echo cancellation. If the experience is unreliable, try again in a different car (if available) to confirm whether it is car-specific.

## Are there limitations to ChatGPT in CarPlay?

ChatGPT in CarPlay supports voice conversations similar to the ChatGPT iOS app, with these limitations:

* It cannot access maps, vehicle information, or live location.
* It cannot control your car or affect other apps (for example, Maps or messaging apps like Mail or Slack).
