# We’re updating our Usage Policies

Review recent updates to OpenAI’s Usage Policies, including stronger protections for minors and safeguards against misuse.

Updated: 6 hours ago

When using OpenAI’s services, you must follow our [Usage Policies](https://openai.com/policies/usage-policies). We update these rules occasionally both to ensure they are not overly restrictive and to strengthen protections for our users. We’re updating these policies to reflect:

* Consolidated Usage Policies that apply to all OpenAI products and services.
* Additional protections prohibiting use of our services to harm minors (under 18).
* A greater focus on protecting people against concrete harms and misuse.
