# ChatGPT iOS and Android app FAQ

Frequently asked questions about the ChatGPT Android app

Updated: 13 days ago

## Overview

Use the ChatGPT app on iPhone, iPad, or Android to view past chats, manage conversations, and adjust app settings.

## FAQ

### Where can I download and install the ChatGPT app?

Download the official app for your device:

* [ChatGPT in the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/openai-chatgpt/id6448311069) for iPhone and iPad. Check that the developer is OpenAI OpCo, LLC.
* [ChatGPT in Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.openai.chatgpt) for Android. Check that the publisher is OpenAI.

If you search the store instead, search for “OpenAI ChatGPT” and check the publisher before downloading.

This is the official app icon:

ChatGPT is also available on Apple Vision Pro through the App Store.

For the supported countries and territories, see: [ChatGPT supported countries](https://help.openai.com/articles/7947663).

### How do I check the ChatGPT app version?

1. Open the sidebar.
2. Select your profile icon to open **Settings**.
3. Find the version for your platform:

* On iOS, scroll to the app version. If your Settings screen has an Information icon (i), select it to view the version.
* On Android, select **About**. The version appears under **ChatGPT for Android**.

### What can I try if the app or its tools freeze or return errors?

For crashes, freezes, and device-check errors, see: [Troubleshooting the ChatGPT mobile app](https://help.openai.com/articles/7910461).

Reinstalling doesn't reset usage limits, change workspace permissions, or remove account restrictions. Follow any limit or access notice shown in ChatGPT.

### How do I access my chat history?

1. Open ChatGPT.
2. Select the menu icon (two horizontal lines) to open the sidebar. Your recent chats appear with the newest first.
3. Select a conversation to reopen it.

Your chat history syncs across devices where you're signed in to the same account. If an older chat isn't in the sidebar, check that you're in the correct account and workspace, then use search. Archived chats remain searchable; locked chats appear under **Projects > Locked chats**.

For the full search steps, see: [Finding your chats, projects, and files in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/articles/10056348).

### How do I delete a chat conversation?

1. Open the conversation you want to delete.
2. Select the more options menu (•••), then **Delete**.
3. Confirm the deletion.

Deleted chats cannot be restored. For more details, see: [Deleting and archiving chats in ChatGPT](https://help.openai.com/articles/8809935).

### How do I delete my account?

Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone. For the consequences and steps on each platform, see: [Deleting your ChatGPT account](https://help.openai.com/articles/6378407).

### How do I select text or use response actions on Android?

Touch and hold response text to select it, then use the Android selection menu.

For ChatGPT response actions, use the buttons below a response or the more options menu (•••). Available actions vary by message and can include **Copy**, **Good response**, **Bad response**, **Read aloud**, **Share**, and **Try again**.

### Can I drag a response into another app on iPad?

Yes. Touch and hold a response, then drag it into another app that accepts text, such as Notes or a spreadsheet.

This example shows a response being dragged into Notes. The apps' appearance may differ on your device.

### How do I turn haptic feedback on or off?

1. Open the sidebar and select your profile icon to open **Settings**.
2. Select **General**.
3. Turn **Haptic feedback** on or off.

If your app has a different Settings layout, look for **Haptic feedback** directly in Settings on iOS, or under **Personalization** on Android. The setting appears only on devices that support the app's haptic effects.
