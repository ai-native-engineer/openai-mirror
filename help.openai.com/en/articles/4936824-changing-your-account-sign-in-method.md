# Changing your account sign-in method

Learn when you can change your sign-in method and how it affects MFA and account access.

Updated: 11 days ago

# Logging in with a different method

* If you originally signed up using an email and password, you can later log in using a social login method (Google, Microsoft, or Apple) as long as the email address matches.
* Your account will automatically combine both login methods, allowing you to log in using either your original email/password or your new social login.

# Social login methods

* If you initially signed up using SSO or a social login method (such as Google, Microsoft, or Apple), you can change your email [by following this article](https://help.openai.com/articles/4936827#for-users-who-signed-up-with-social-login)**.** If you are an Enterprise customer, your account director can reset pre-authorization to enable username/password login.
* Note that if you signed up with **Sign in with Apple** and chose **Hide My Email**, your account will be tied to Apple’s private relay email address instead of your personal email. To access the original account, continue using **Sign in with Apple** with the same Apple ID; using your personal email or another social login method will open a different account.

# Managing multi-factor authentication (MFA)

* MFA is tied to the login method you used when setting it up.
* To switch your login method after enabling MFA:

  1. **Disable MFA first.**
  2. Log out, then log back in using the new sign-in method.
  3. **Re-enable MFA** with your new login method.

⚠️ **IMPORTANT NOTE**: Once MFA is turned on, you must continue using your current login method until MFA is disabled.

# Deleting your account

Deleting your account is permanent and does not change its sign-in method. If you signed up with Google, Apple, or Microsoft, you may be able to add a password to your existing account without deleting it:

* Sign in using your original sign-in method.
* Open **Settings** and select **Security and login**.
* If **Password** shows **Add**, select **Add** and follow the instructions.

If you don’t see this option, your account may not be eligible.

⚠️ **IMPORTANT NOTE:** Deleting your account does **NOT** allow you to change your authentication method.
