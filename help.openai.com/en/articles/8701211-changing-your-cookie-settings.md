# Changing your cookie settings

Learn how to change your cookie settings on ChatGPT, OpenAI, and the API platform.

Updated: 13 days ago

Depending on your region, you may be able to choose which cookies are set.

# For Help Center (help.openai.com)

1. Go to help.openai.com.
2. Scroll to the bottom of the page.
3. Select **Cookie Preferences** in the footer.

# For ChatGPT (chatgpt.com)

1. Go to chatgpt.com.
2. Scroll to the bottom of the page.
3. Select **Cookie Preferences** in the footer.

# For OpenAI (openai.com)

1. Go to openai.com.
2. Scroll to the bottom of the page.
3. Select **Manage Cookies** in the footer.

# For Platform (platform.openai.com)

You can manage cookies while logged in or logged out.

While logged in:

1. Log in to your account.
2. Select your profile icon.
3. Select **Manage cookies**.

While logged out:

1. Open the menu (2 horizontal lines).
2. Select **Manage cookies**.

The **Manage cookies** screen shows the available options.
