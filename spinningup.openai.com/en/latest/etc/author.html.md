# About the Author[¶](#about-the-author "Permalink to this headline")

Spinning Up in Deep RL was primarily developed by Josh Achiam, a research scientist on the OpenAI Safety Team and PhD student at UC Berkeley advised by Pieter Abbeel. Josh studies topics related to safety in deep reinforcement learning, and has previously published work on [safe exploration](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.10528).
