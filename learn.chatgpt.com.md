# Bring your ideas to life.

ChatGPT can now take on your most ambitious work. The familiar ChatGPT
experience and the full power of Codex now in one app.

[Download ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/download/)[Get started](/docs/quickstart)

## Explore ideas.

Use Chat to ask questions, think through possibilities, and make sense of topics through conversation.

Learn something new

Ask questions, get clear explanations, and follow your curiosity through conversation.

Brainstorm and develop ideas

Turn a rough thought into stronger ideas you can explore and refine.

Compare options and make plans

Understand the tradeoffs, choose a direction, and turn it into practical next steps.

## Get work done.

Use ChatGPT Work to turn a goal, files, and context into documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and other useful deliverables.

Research and synthesize information

Bring together files and sources, then turn the important details into a clear point of view.

Analyze files and data

Find patterns, compare evidence, and surface the insights that matter.

Create documents, spreadsheets, and presentations

Turn your source material into polished work you can review, share, and keep refining.

[Learn more](/docs/features)

## Build anything.

Use Codex to understand codebases, build and test features, fix bugs, and review changes.

Understand and navigate a codebase

Understand how a project is structured, find the important files, and get oriented quickly.

Build features and fix bugs

Turn an idea or issue into a working change, then iterate on the result.

Test, review, and ship changes

Make focused changes, run checks, review the diff, and get the work ready to ship.
