Why the OpenAI Foundation

AI has extraordinary potential to accelerate scientific discovery, expand opportunity, and help address some of society’s hardest problems. Realizing those benefits—and preparing for the challenges advanced AI may bring—requires sustained investment and collaboration.

The OpenAI Foundation supports research, partnerships, and programs that help translate technological progress into broad public benefit.

Who we’re looking for

The opportunities and challenges created by advanced AI span many disciplines. We welcome people with backgrounds in science, philanthropy, public policy, operations, finance, partnerships, and nonprofit leadership.

We’re looking for experts, operators, and program leaders who bring curiosity, sound judgment, and a commitment to public benefit.
